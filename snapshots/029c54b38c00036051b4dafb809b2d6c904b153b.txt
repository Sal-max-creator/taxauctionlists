Tax Sales
DELINQUENT TAX LIST
The delinquent tax list will be posted on the County's website following publication in the Village Voices on October 28, 2026.
When will your next tax sale be held?
Pursuant to Public Act 104-0553, the Stephenson County Board has approved Resolution 26-09-119 .
The County, as Trustee, will acquire the tax certificates for all unpaid parcels as of the date of the tax sale scheduled for November 10, 2026. The November 2026 sale of unpaid 2025 property taxes is not open to private tax buyers.
The resolution refers to an attached list of unpaid parcels. That list will be shared on this page following publication in the Village Voices on October 28, 2026.
Statutory references within 26-09-119.
Do you have any sales scheduled after the next one coming up?
The tax sale only occurs once per year.
The separate, annual auction of surplus property also occurs once per year during the summer.
Booklets for the 2027 Surplus Property sealed-bid auction will be available in late July, 2027 . Electronic booklets may be purchased online at Illinois Tax Deeds | Illinois Tax Liens.
Will all other liens be cleared from the property as a result of the sale?
No, the tax sale does not clear any liens from the property. Only delinquent TAXES are sold at the tax sale.
No actual property is sold.
Is a copy of the county and state statutes and rules regarding the tax sale available for purchase?
The Illinois Compiled Statutes are available at your local library or online at www.ilga.gov .
Where do I call for redemption information?
Please contact the Stephenson County Clerk's Office at 815-235-8289.
