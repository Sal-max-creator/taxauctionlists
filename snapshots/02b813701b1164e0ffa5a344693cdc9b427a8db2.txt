Tax Buyer
Madison County will conduct its Annual Mobile Home Tax Sale at 10:00 a.m. on Feb. 16, 2027 in the Madison County Board Room (Ste. 203) 157 N. Main St., Edwardsville.
Deadline for registration is 4:30 p.m. on Jan 29 , 2027
Complete and submit the Madison County Tax Buyer Registration Form along with a W-9 form .
Submit a deposit of $500 payable to the "Madison County Treasurer." Your deposit will be applied to your purchase at the Tax Sale or refunded if you attend and do not make a purchase. Your deposit is forfeited if you do not attend the sale.
Tax Buyer Lists are available starting January 22, 2027.
By law, you must notify the Treasurer's Office by mail, fax or email, no later than Feb. 5, 2027 for the name of your substitute bidder.
The Tax Sale is conducted by Joseph E. Meyer & Associates using R.A.M.S. 2 or Randomize Auction Management System. If you have any questions about R.A.M.S 2 please contact Joseph E. Meyer & Associates.
