Tax Possessions and Upcoming Auctions
A "Tax Possession" is a property held by the City of Fall River after the Land Court Department of the Trial Court has entered a Decree of Foreclosure pursuant to the City's filing of a Petition to Foreclose, or a property that has been deeded to the City in Lieu of Foreclosure. Once a parcel becomes a Tax Possession the City holds it free and clear of all mortgages, liens, and attachments. Less than 1% of all the tax parcels in the City of Fall River will ever become a Tax Possession.
Pursuant to Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 60, &sect; 77B the Custodian of Tax Possessions shall have the care, custody, management, and control of all Tax Possessions acquired by the City of Fall River. The Custodian of Tax Possessions is appointed by the Mayor. In Fall River, the Custodian of Tax Possessions has traditionally been the City Administrator. Further, pursuant to Section 77B, the Custodian of Tax Possessions, acting on behalf of the City of Fall River, may, notwithstanding any provision of law, ordinance, or by-law inconsistent herewith, sell at public auction any Tax Possession of the City of Fall River.
If a Tax Possession contains less than the minimum square footage required to be considered a buildable lot in the City of Fall River it may be offered for sale through the Abutters Lots Program. For more information about the City's Abutters Lots Program please see Section 42-144 of the City of Fall River Code of ordinances.
Pursuant to Section 42-146 of the City of Fall River Code of Ordinance, parcels for which a decree of foreclosure or deed in lieu has been received by the Treasurer between January 1 and June 30, and all parcels remaining from the prior Abutters Lots Program sale, shall be auctioned in the following October. All parcels for which a decree of foreclosure or deed in lieu has been received by the Treasurer between July 1 and December 31, and all parcels remaining from the prior Abutters Lots Program sale shall be auctioned in the following April. All auctions are advertised on the City's website and in the Fall River.
City of Fall River
Tax Possession Auction
October 29, 2025
The City of Fall River (hereinafter "the City"), acting through its Custodian of Tax Possessions, will auction and sell at Public Auction the Tax Possession listed below. The successful bidder will purchase said Tax Possession "as is" and no warranties or representations are made regarding said Tax Possession. This Tax Possession Auction will begin at 11:00 am on October 29, 2025 at 243 Lynwood Street, Fall River, Massachusetts. It is being held pursuant to the provisions of Massachusetts General Laws Chapter 60, Section 77B. A Site visit will be conducted on October 29, 2025 at 10:00 am.
The terms of sale are as follows: Bidders will be required to present a certified check or money order in the amount of $15,000.00 to be qualified to bid. Deposits made by unsuccessful bidders will be returned to the bidder following the completion of the auction. The successful bidder will be required to pay the balance of his or her bid within thirty (30) days after the date of the auction by bank, certified check or wired funds. Said payment shall be made at the offices of the Treasurer at One Government Center, Fall River, Massachusetts. The City reserves the right to sell a Tax Possession to the second highest bidder if the original highest bidder fails to perform within the required time period.
The City of Fall River reserves the right to remove any Tax Possession from the list of parcels to be auctioned at any time prior to the commencement of the Auction or during said Auction. The Custodian of Tax Possession reserves the right to reject all bids.
All requirements of Massachusetts General Laws, Chapter 60 shall be complied with. Inquiries or requests for further information regarding this Tax Possession Auction can be made at the Treasurer's Office Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. through 4:30 p.m. or by calling (508) 324-2260 or by visiting the City of Fall River's Website at www.fallriverma.gov .
The City of Fall River advises all prospective bidders to seek legal advice regarding the nature of tax possessions and the auction process prior to bidding at the Auction.
The following parcel shall be auctioned:
Parcel I
243 Lynwood Street
Parcel ID: C17-0112

to announce additional terms at the time of auction.
