Delinquent Tax Sale - Foreclosures
The Snohomish County Treasurer conducts an ANNUAL Delinquent Tax Foreclosure Auction, pursuant to RCW CHAPTER 84.64. This annual sale of property includes real property with one full year of taxes that are three years old and delinquent, meaning if the full year 2023 or any prior years are unpaid as of January 2026 it makes a property eligible for Tax Sale in December 2026.
DUE TO COURT HEARING DATE CONFLICTS, the 2026 Snohomish County Tax Foreclosure Sale Auction date is now tentatively set for THURSDAY DECEMBER 3, 2026 and will be held online at https://www.bid4assets.com / Final date for the sale are always determined by the Final Order of Sale filed with the court, mid-November 2026.
FOR A CURRENT LISTING OF THE PROPERTIES STILL SUBJECT TO the 2026 Tax Sale - please go to the Bid4Assets website and view the SPECIALTY CHANNELS &lsquo;County Tax Sales' for the &lsquo;Snohomish County, WA Tax Foreclosed Properties Auction' https://www.bid4assets.com /
The 2026 Certificate of Delinquency & Exhibit A, Summons & Notice, Complaint & Servicemembers Notice of Protection were filed in Snohomish County Superior Court on 5/14/2026- Case #26-2-04940-31.
The 2026 Affidavit of Publication was filed in Superior Court on 14th day of September, 2026 and is available on the &lsquo;Request for Foreclosure Information' page. This is the published notice of parties with a recorded interest in property subject to Tax Foreclosure. This would show a list of property still at risk for tax foreclosure- as of the date it was filed in court. See the Bid4Assets website for more updated information.
HOMEOWNERS/TAXPAYERS/RECORDED INTEREST LIEN HOLDERS/parties with recorded interest with property in the 2026 TAX FORECLOSURE : You have until WEDNESDAY DECEMBER 2, 2026 at 4:59pm to submit full payment IN OUR OFFICE by certified funds (No postmark accepted!) and avoid Tax Foreclosure and sale of your property. You will be required to:
Pay ALL the property taxes, interest, penalty, applicable assessments and foreclosure costs-CALL FOR AMOUNT DUE.
Payment must be in certified funds. Acceptable forms of payments are-cashier's check, money orders, cash by appointment only. Any other forms of payment- personal/business checks, on-line payments, credit/debit card payments WILL BE RETURNED, with no exceptions.
Payment must be from the legal owner/taxpayer listed on the Tax Roll, or a recorded interest party ONLY. Any party paying of behalf of a taxpayer as their agent will be required to provide notarized documentation of the agency relationship- per RCW 84.64.060. Call the Foreclosure division for directions first.
Homeowners in Foreclosure- your first step is to contact the Revenue Officer-Foreclosure at 425 388-3606. We can advise you of your current situation and offer any taxpayer assistance allowed by law. PLEASE contact us to discuss these options. You should also contact the Washington State Homeownership Resource Center hotline at 877-894-4663 or visit their website https://www.homeownership-wa.org/ for help with resources to find out all your options with homeownership and foreclosure. They may be able to connect you to legal advisors and/or rescue loan services.
Prospective BUYERS OF TAX PROPERTIES:
The tax sale &lsquo;property list', minimum bid amounts, Terms of Sale, and property information IS NOW AVAILABLE on the Bid4Assets web pages, along with links to Snohomish Co. Assessor maps. Title reports are for Foreclosure notice purpose and use, and are NOT to be relied on for personal use or as a basis for determining whether or not to bid on a parcel. THE PARCELS ARE OFFERED ON A &lsquo;WHERE IS' AND &lsquo;AS IS' BASIS, AND SNOHOMISH COUNTY MAKES NO REPRESENTATION OF WARRANTY, EXPRESSED OR IMPLIED, NOR ANY GUARANTEE OF WARRANTY, EXPRESSED OR IMPLIED, AS TO THE CONDITION OF TITLE TO ANY PROPERTY NOR THE PHYSICAL CONDITION OF ANY PROPERTY OR ITS FITNESS FOR ANY USE OR PURPOSE. NOTE- these are private properties until sold at the Tax Sale, so please respect all privacy rights the private owners/residents have.
If property taxes are paid in full by the CURRENT OWNER between the posting of the list on Bid4Assets.com and the sale date, they will be noted as "WITHDRAWN" on the Bid4Assets Auction folder. The Auction folders are updated every day or so to keep you current, and the morning of the actual sale date. The Excel list on Bid4Assets does NOT get updated .
You must look at each parcel under the &lsquo;Auction Folder' to see if it has been Withdrawn at ANY TIME before the sale.
You MUST PRE-REGISTER & pay a pre-deposit to be eligible to bid at the sale. See Bid4Assets for more information.
YOU MUST READ AND AGREE TO the 2026 Snohomish County TERMS OF SALE on Bid4Assets.
Please see the "Frequently Asked Questions" page for more information.
Public record requests for &lsquo;Foreclosure Lists' or &lsquo;Foreclosure Information' -prior or current year-please see the &lsquo;Request for Foreclosure Information' page. NOTE-there are multiple "lists" of properties on our webpage under Requests for Foreclosure Information, and they are created as the County needs them for foreclosure purposes, NOT because you request them- but they are available without a Public Record request- as long as you agree to the disclaimer you are required to read before you access them.
Note: Property that is foreclosed by banks, mortgage companies and other lenders is often sold at &lsquo;Trustee's Sale' or &lsquo;Sheriff Sale' ON THE SNOHOMISH COUNTY COURTHOUSE STEPS ON FRIDAYS . The Treasurer office does NOT have information on those lending agency foreclosures. You may search the Snohomish County Auditor/Recorded Documents for a "Notice of Trustee Sale" and contact the Trustee (the business processing the Foreclosure sale) for information about their sales. For questions regarding the surplus funds generated by these sales, contact the Clerk of the Snohomish County Superior Court at 425 388-3466 (Trustee Sale) or the Sheriff Civil Unit at 425 388-3522 (Sheriff Levy Sale).
