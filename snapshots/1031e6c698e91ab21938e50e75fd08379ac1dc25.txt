Property TaxesTax Data Request
To view and pay PROPERTY TAXES online Click HERE
The deadline for paying your property taxes is January 31st.
If you did not receive your tax statement, please contact the Tax Office
The delinquency date is February 1st.
Failure to receive your bill does not allow for penalty and interest to be waived
Auto Registration
To REGISTER your automobile or trailer online, with a credit card, Click HERE Version OptionsCounty Tax Assessor / CollectorHeadline Auto Registration .
Title and Reg.
Trailers without a VIN# (Vehicle Identification Number), cannot be registered online.
Please contact The Henderson County Appraisal District, at 903-675-9296, for the following:
To protest your property value and notify them of changes to property
To apply for exemptions, or to have exemptions removed
For changes or corrections to the Ownership of property on the tax roll
For information on Delinquent Tax Sales, you&apos;ll need to contact the delinquent Tax Attorneys below:
McCreary, Veselka, Bragg and Allen, at 903-675-6182, or go to their website at www.mvbalaw.com .
Linebarger, Goggan, Blair and Sampson, at 903-597-2897, or go to their website at https://www.lgbs.com .
**A person purchasing property at the sale will be required to present to the officer who conducts the sale a written statement from the County Tax Assessor/Collector that the purchaser does not owe any delinquent taxes in that county.
Please come to our main office in Athens, to request this statement. The cost is $10 and it is valid for 90 days.
You must present a photo ID at that time.
