Property Tax Information
NOTICE - Under the new property tax bill passed by the 2025 Montana Legislature, mobile home owners may see a significant increase in their property tax bills. More information on this issue is available here .
First-half 2025 Real Estate Tax bills were due December 1, 2025. Second-half payments will be due on June 1, 2026.
PLEASE CALL 406-447-8329 FOR PAYOFF ON ALL DELINQUENT REAL ESTATE TAXES.
* Please note: Legal descriptions on this website may be incomplete. Complete legal descriptions can be obtained from your current deed or from the Clerk and Recorder's office, Room 113 at 316 North Park Avenue, Helena, Montana.
Homestead and Long-term Rental Reduced Tax Rate
The Montana Department of Revenue Homestead and Long-term Rental enrollment portal is now open for 2027 tax year applications.
To check your status, find more information, or apply for the reduced tax rate, visit Montana Department of Revenue Homestead and Long-Term Rentals
Tax Inquiry (Property Tax Lookup)
Search by name, parcel number, tax id number, address, geo code, subcode, or section, township, and range. (Note: you may only search with one field at a time.)
Data is updated nightly from the Lewis and Clark Treasurer's tax/parcel records.
Any and all information displayed is 'public information'.

Make Your Payment
By Mail
Mail In Your Tax Payment
Property tax payments can be mailed to:
Property Tax Department
Lewis & Clark County
316 North Park Avenue Rm 113
Helena, Montana 59623
Please make checks to "County Treasurer."
To look up the amount due for your property, follow our Tax Inquiry Property Tax Lookup information above.
Drop Off or In-Person
You can drop off your property tax payment in drop boxes located inside each entrance of the City-County, 316 N Park Ave, downtown Helena.
You can also visit our staff in Rm 113 to pay your taxes in person. Our office is located on the first floor at the back of the City-County building (near the west entry/exit).
Online
Pay Your Taxes Online
Go to https://itax.tylerapp.com/LewisAndClarkMT (clicking this link will take you to our third-party payment provider).
Search for your property. (You may only search with one field at a time.)
Select your property.
Click on the Pay Taxes button and Add to Cart.
Select which half you are paying. (To pay both, add one half then the other.)
Continue to Cart and proceed to Checkout.
You will be taken to the Official Payments site. Enter your credit or debit card information and process your payment.*
*Additional fees will apply. Payments are uploaded into our system every two hours.
Click here for step-by-step online payment instructions with screenshots.(PDF, 1MB)
By Phone
Call toll free 888-272-9829 .
You will be asked for a jurisdiction number. The jurisdiction number for Lewis and Clark County is 3602.
You will also be asked to provide your Property Tax ID number. This number is located on your property tax payment stubs. If you don't know your Property Tax ID number, you may search for it at https://itax.tylerapp.com/LewisAndClarkMT (clicking this link will take you to our authorized third-party provider).
You will be prompted to provide additional information, such as your credit or debit card payment information.*
*Additional fees will apply.
Alternative Payment Schedule for Primary Residences
The Alternative Payment Schedule for Primary Residences allows homeowners to pay their current property taxes on their primary residence through a seven-payment installment plan instead of the standard schedule. To participate:
The homeowner must apply (PDF, 146KB) to the county treasurer by September 30 and swear that the property is their primary residence (occupied at least 7 months of the year).
Once enrolled, participation continues automatically each year unless the owner requests termination in writing before September 30.
Payment must be made online and will include a service fee from our third-party payment platform.
Early payments are allowed, but third-party services (like mortgage companies or escrow accounts) cannot make payments under this schedule.
The law defines a "primary residence" as an owned and occupied home (including single-family, multi-unit, trailer, or mobile home) on a permanent foundation classified as class four residential property .

Delinquent Tax Process
For information on tax liens from 2015 or prior, please refer to Montana Code Annotated, Title 15, Chapters 16, 17, and 18 . For information on tax liens from 2016 going forward, please refer to House Bill 18(PDF, 134KB) . For information on tax liens for 2018 and forward please refer to Senate Bill 253(PDF, 179KB) .
It is the responsibility of the interested person to conduct their own research of the delinquent tax process prior to purchasing any assignments. If you have questions in regards to tax liens, please consult an attorney. Lewis and Clark County will not provide legal advice of any kind.
The Lewis and Clark County Treasurer's office does not conduct tax sale assignment business during the tax season months of November and May. Tax sale assignment business resumes when the processing of current postmarked mail is complete.
