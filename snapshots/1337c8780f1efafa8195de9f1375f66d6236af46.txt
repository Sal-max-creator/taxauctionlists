Location & Time
Foreclosure sales are held online . Tax sales occur in person .
Real Estate Tax Sales are held on the first floor lobby area of the Hayes Services Building (145 N. Union St, Delaware, Ohio 43015) on Wednesdays at 10:00AM (see schedule below).
Foreclosure sales run through the RealAuction website, https://delaware.sheriffsaleauction.ohio.gov/ . Foreclosure sales run on that website on Wednesdays starting at 10:00 AM (see schedule below). If this page does not address your need, please call our Sheriff&#8217;s Sales Coordinator at (740) 833-2868.
You may obtain more information on Sheriff&#8217;s Sales via:
Email sheriffsales@co.delaware.oh.us
Delaware County Auditor&#8217;s Office
Delaware County Clerk of Courts Office
Terms
Minimum bid of 2/3 of the appraised value unless otherwise noted.
Balance due within 30 days of the date that the confirmation is filed with the Clerk of Courts.
Deposit is due at the time of sale in the form of Cash or a Certified Check for tax sales. For foreclosure sales, RealAuction requires deposit at least 2 days prior to the sale by wire or 5 days prior by ACH.
Please print and legibly fill out the Purchaser Information Form and bring it to the auction (if the form is not legible, it will be returned) for tax sales.
Learn more a bout online real estate foreclosures.
