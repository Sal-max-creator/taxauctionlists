PHILADELPHIA — Media Advisory
Innovative public tool represents Phase II of Sheriff Rochelle Bilal’s modernization and reorganization strategy
PHILADELPHIA, PA — The Philadelphia Sheriff’s Office will announce the upcoming launch of its new Philadelphia Sheriff’s Auction Search Platform, an innovative public resource designed to help prospective bidders more easily search, understand, and prepare for Philadelphia Sheriff’s property auctions before participating through the official online bidding platform at Bid4Assets.com/Philadelphia.
The new platform represents Phase II of Sheriff Rochelle Bilal’s comprehensive reorganization and modernization strategy aimed at improving transparency, customer service, and public access to Sheriff Sale information. The platform serves as an educational and informational gateway, allowing users to search available properties, access instructional resources, save auction listings, and better understand the auction process before placing bids through Bid4Assets.
The announcement follows Phase I of the Sheriff’s reorganization initiative, highlighted in Sheriff Bilal’s recent City & State Pennsylvania op-ed, which outlined the progress the Office has made in modernizing operations, improving accountability, and delivering more efficient public services.
“Our commitment has always been to make government more transparent, accessible, and accountable to the people we serve,” said Sheriff Rochelle Bilal.
“This new Auction Search Platform removes many of the barriers that have traditionally made Sheriff’s Sales difficult to understand. Whether you’re a first-time homebuyer, investor, attorney, or simply someone interested in learning more about the process, this platform provides the information and educational tools necessary to participate with confidence while maintaining the integrity of the official bidding process through Bid4Assets.”
Unlike traditional auction websites, the Philadelphia Sheriff’s Auction Search Platform is designed as an informational resource—not a bidding website. The platform gives users access to comprehensive search tools and educational content while directing all official bidding activity to the secure Bid4Assets platform.
Click here to view the how-to video: https://youtu.be/ESj4nJu5ICE
Key Features Include:
Search available Sheriff Sale properties by auction date, property address, Auction ID, OPA number, Book/Writ, ZIP code, or keyword.
Browse properties through both searchable lists and interactive map views.
Create personalized user profiles to save and organize properties of interest.
Watch instructional videos on your desktop/mobile devices explaining the Sheriff Sale process and how to participate in online auctions.
View official terms and conditions and other important auction documents.
Access homeowner assistance resources for individuals seeking information on foreclosure prevention and saving their property.
Seamlessly connect to Bid4Assets to complete the official online bidding process.
The platform also incorporates enhanced consumer protections by requiring users to review educational materials and acknowledge important terms before creating an account.
Throughout the experience, users are reminded that the Philadelphia Sheriff’s Office website is an informational resource and that all live bidding takes place exclusively through Bid4Assets.
The Auction Search Platform is part of the Sheriff’s Office’s broader technology modernization initiative, which has focused on improving customer service, increasing transparency, streamlining operations, and making government services more accessible to Philadelphia residents.
“Innovation is not just about technology—it’s about creating better public service,” Bilal added. “This platform reflects our commitment to educating the public, improving access to information, and ensuring every participant has the tools they need to make informed decisions.”
The Philadelphia Sheriff’s Office expects the new platform to become a valuable resource for homebuyers, investors, attorneys, financial institutions, real estate professionals, and members of the public interested in participating in Sheriff’s Sales.
Additional phases of the Sheriff’s modernization initiative will continue to roll out in the coming months as part of the Office’s long-term commitment to improving government services and public engagement.

About the Philadelphia Sheriff's Office -
The Philadelphia Sheriff’s Office wants to ensure that Philadelphia residents can remain in their homes, which is why it supports programs that help residents learn more about available resources. Philadelphia residents must contact the Sheriff’s Office if they believe they have been illegally evicted by someone who was impersonating a Deputy Sheriff. The Sheriff’s Office will also continue to host food giveaways and other related activities to respond to the needs of all residents. If there are any questions that a resident may have, they can reach out to the Philadelphia Sheriff’s Office at 215-686-3560 or 215-686-3542 or on our website, phillysheriff.com .
