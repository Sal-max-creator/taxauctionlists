Registering your account allows you to schedule payments, receive text and email notifications, and view your account history.
The Town of Bloomfield Tax Collector&apos;s Office is responsible for the prompt and efficient billing and collection of real estate, personal property, and motor vehicle taxes for all current and prior Grand Lists, ensuring the necessary revenue is secured for essential town services. Additionally, through a contractual agreement, we collect on behalf of the Bloomfield Center and Blue Hills Fire Districts.
We strive to uphold fairness and integrity for every taxpayer in accordance with the Connecticut General Statutes.
