It is the bidder&apos;s sole responsibility to make any determination for usability and fitness for any intended use. This sale will be conducted virtually on the Public Surplus site . Please make sure to read the Terms and Conditions of this sale thoroughly.

Additional Auction Information
Under RCW 84.64.080 "No County Officer or Employee may directly or indirectly be a purchaser of the property at the sale."; by bidding on any property, you attest that you are not a county officer or employee or bidding on behalf, by or through a county officer or employee. Anyone found bidding as such will be disqualified from the current sale and all future sales.
You must become a registered bidder through Public Surplus in order to bid on this auction, if you have questions or issues please use the chat feature or reach out to Public Surplus directly. We do not manage the website and will not respond to emails or inquiries regarding the website or setting up of your public surplus account.
Viewing only period on Public Surplus is until the sale begins. You cannot bid prior to the sale opening. Please pay attention to the start and end times of the auction. We will not respond to emails and inquiries regarding this process.
Each property will have a $500 deposit required to bid. You will be prompted to do this at the time you place your first bid (after the sale is open). This is the only payment accepted by debit/credit card. All deposit funds will be returned unless you default on your purchase. This credit card hold will not go toward the purchase price of the property. It will be released by the vendor.
Each property will have a $350* Administrative fee (subject to change) added to the price of the sale which covers: deed preparation, excise preparation, excise fee, and recording fee. *subject to change
Public Surplus charges a buyer premium of 5% of the total sale price, payable by the buyer at closing.
Final payments will be accepted by Wire Transfer only , winning bidders will have 3 business days to complete this transaction or they will be in default. PAYMENT DEADLINE IS TUESDAY, NOVEMBER 24, 2026 @ 5:00PM! If unpaid, you are considered defaulted and will lose your deposit.
Bidding will begin for all parcels at 9 am November 17, 2026, and will end at 9 pm November 19, 2026; We will not respond to emails or inquiries asking why bids cannot be placed prior to the auction opening.
Treasurer reserves the right to reject any bid or offer, and reserves the right to pull any property from the sale up to and including after the bidding has ended.
Treasurer further reserves the right to relist or reoffer any property as necessary to complete the sale process.
