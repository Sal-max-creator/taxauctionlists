Tax Payment Options
The Cochise County Treasurer&apos;s Office processes payments via Certified Payments and does not receive any portion of the convenience fee. For online payments, the Cochise County Treasurer is not permitted to charge additional fees. In addition, the Treasurer is not allowed to deduct banking fees from the property tax (levy) collected, which would result in apportioning a lesser amount to the taxing jurisdictions. Unlike private-sector merchants, the Treasurer&apos;s Office cannot absorb processing fees into the cost of doing business.
For e-checks, please be careful when entering routing and account number information. If an incorrect routing number or account number is used, your payment will be returned within five days. The Treasurer&apos;s office will charge a $25 service fee for all returned checks. To pay multiple parcels or multiple years, select "Add More Payments to Cart" on Step 2 of the payment process and follow the instructions provided.
If you have mailed in a payment please do not make a duplicate payment online or over the phone. Requests for reversal of duplicate payments are $20. Cochise County has over 100,000 parcels, please be patient while we process payments as quickly as possible. Thank you!
Please note : Tax Liens cannot be purchased online. Funds need to be sent/mailed to the Treasurer&apos;s office.

Phone / Online Payments
Please note : Payments made online or over the phone will take up to a week to post to the Treasurer Parcel Inquiry site. All payments will be posted using the date paid.
To pay by phone please call 800-231-6792. This number provides a live agent 24 hours a day, 7 days a week (phone payments only).
You will be redirected to Treasurer Parcel Inquiry where you will enter your parcel number. Once you enter your parcel number you will find the Pay Online option on the left side at the bottom of the menu.
For problems or issues with the online payment system, please call 520-432-8400 between the hours of 8 am and 5 pm MST, Monday through Friday.
Mail Your Payment
Mailed payments must be postmarked on or before the delinquent date. It is recommended that payments made near the delinquent date are walked into the post office. You may send payments made with a check or money order using the envelope and coupon provided with the tax notice.

Back Tax
Payment for back taxes must be made in the form of cashier&apos;s check, money order, or cash. Back taxes can also be paid using credit or debit cards and can be paid online or over the phone.
Back tax amounts cannot be given out over the phone and must be requested in writing to the Cochise County Treasurer&apos;s Office. Requests can be emailed to the Treasurer or mailed to:
Cochise County Treasurer
P.O. Box 1778
Bisbee, AZ 85603
The request must include the parcel number, name on the property, and your contact information.
If you are redeeming a property on behalf of the owner, an Affidavit of Redemption (PDF) is required .
Tax Liens
By law, the Treasurer can no longer process administrative foreclosure for Treasurer deeds. The Certificate Holder must obtain a Judicial foreclosure for the issuance of Treasurer&apos;s deeds.
The map and list of available tax liens is updated nightly. If you have any questions about the map please contact the Cochise County Treasurer&apos;s Office. Please do not contact the Geographic Information Systems (GIS) Department.
If you are interested in purchasing a tax lien, you will need to fill out a Bidder Application (PDF) . Please make sure to use our Treasurer Parcel Inquiry to find information about each parcel.
To speak to someone in the Back Tax Department please call 520-432-8400 and select option 3 and then option 2 for back tax. You can also email the Back Tax Department .

Responsibilities and Mission
Treasurer Responsibilities
The Cochise County Treasurer is the fiscal custodian of the County and is responsible for the administration and control of cash and securities. The duties involved in this responsibility include tax administration; receipt, deposit and disbursement of cash; supervision of county bank accounts; cash flow projections; investment of idle funds; debt management of bond issues, lines of credit and registered warrants. The County Treasurer plays a crucial role in county government administration.
Mission
The Mission of the Cochise County Treasurer is:
To exercise prudence and integrity in the management of all County funds
To strive to carry out the programs and goals of an Arizona Constitutional office in a cost efficient manner while meeting the statutory and administrative requirements
To provide quality service as Treasurer, tax collector, cash manager and banker for all county agencies, school districts and special districts. To receipt, guard, invest, balance and report all county funds and to pay them out on proper authority
To have reliable information available for the Board of Supervisors, auditors, county agencies, school districts, special districts and the citizens of Cochise County
To continue to learn and develop new and more efficient ways to provide courteous, professional service
To represent county constituents before the state legislature, state agencies and at public functions
To enhance and promote the professional management of County government for the public benefit
Important Notice to Taxpayers:
If you receive correspondence regarding your property taxes from anyone other than the Cochise County Treasurer&apos;s Office, please call our office at 520-432-8400 to verify the validity of the information being sent to you. The Cochise County Treasurer&apos;s Office has the most current and accurate information regarding the status of your property taxes.
