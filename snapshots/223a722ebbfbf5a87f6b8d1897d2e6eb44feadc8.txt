Tax Billing and Collections
Collection Division
Payment for Taxes may continue to be made online , by mail, or in-person at the Collections Office. Online payments will have an additional 3.75% fee charged to the credit card. E-check payments carry a flat fee of $1.95. We continue to honor postmark if paying by mail. Parking Tickets can be paid by credit card at the Newport Police Department, by cash or check at the Collections office, or by mail. Payments made online at the website noted on the back of the ticket will carry a flat fee of $3.95.
Collection Division Personnel are charged with the responsibility of receiving, posting and depositing funds for Real Estate, Motor Vehicle Excise and Personal Property taxes, Parking Tickets and Municipal Court Fees. Payments for Trash/Recycle Carts are also paid for in the Collections Office. Parking program permits that are issued from the office include Resident Stickers, Guest Pass(es) & decals, Ledge Road Fishing Permits, King Park automobile & trailer permits.
IMPORTANT NOTICE: UTILITY BILLING SYSTEM TRANSITION
The Department of Utilities is launching a new billing system. As part of this transition, the Collection Division will NO LONGER accept utility bill payments starting July 31 st , 2025 . As of July 31 st , 2025 taxes and water bill payments must be made separately.
Starting with your August Bill , all utility payments can be made online through the new online billing portal . Your mailed August bill will contain your updated account number and new CID number, which you will need to set up your new portal login.
What You'll Need to Do When the New System Goes Live​
Create a new login - Once you have your mailed August bill, view the New Login Set Up Guide (your billing history will transfer over)!
Set up your payment information
Re-enroll in autopay
New Options:
Enroll in paperless billing & pay by text
Please visit the Department of Utilities, Billing Office webpage for more payment options and instructions. If you have questions about the new system
you can contact the Department of Utilities billing staff by phone (401-845-5604) or email ( utilitybilling@cityofnewport.com ).
Please Note
*For questions about utility bill payments made PRIOR to July 31 st , 2025 , contact the Collection Division at 401-845-5401

Municipal Court
The City of Newport established a Municipal Court system in 1987. It is presided over by one justice who adjudicates cases relative to City ordinance, parking and other traffic violations.
Court sessions are held each Monday (except Holidays) in the Council Chambers at City Hall at 1:30 p.m.
The Municipal Court Administrator's office is located in the Collection Office on the first floor and maintains the same hours of operation -- 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.. If you need further information, please call 401 845-5410
Fines may be paid at that location during normal business hours.
Tax Sale - General Information
The City of Newport holds one annual Tax Lien Sale for delinquent taxes on real properties situated in the City of Newport, generally during the month of August.
The sale is held in accordance with Chapter 9 of the Rhode Island General Laws. Successful bidders at the sale will be issued a Tax Collector's Deed, which will be recorded in the Office of Land Evidence within the statutory time limit.
