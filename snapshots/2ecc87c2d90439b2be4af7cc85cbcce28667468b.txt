NOTICE ONLINE SHERIFF SALES
On September 13, 2022 the Mahoning County Sheriff's Office will conduct the sale of all real estate subject to foreclosure on the "Official Public Sheriff's Sale Website" which is operated by Realauction based on a contract with the Ohio Department of Administrative Services. The process and procedures under which these sales will be completed are detailed at the Realauction website https://Mahoning.sheriffsaleauction.ohio.gov. All prospective bidders should familiarize themselves with this new process.
