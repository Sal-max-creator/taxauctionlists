Tax Foreclosure Properties
Ward County administers the tax foreclosure process for real estate with delinquent property taxes. Properties that are not redeemed before the applicable deadline may be acquired by Ward County through tax foreclosure and offered for sale.
Tax Foreclosure Process
Real estate may become subject to tax foreclosure when property taxes, special assessments, interest, and penalties remain delinquent.
Property owners have until October 1 to pay the taxes for the year subject to foreclosure. If October 1 falls on a weekend or holiday, the deadline moves to the next business day.
After the October 1 deadline, all three years of outstanding taxes must be paid to prevent the property from proceeding through the tax foreclosure process.
Property owners with questions regarding the amount required to prevent foreclosure should contact the Ward County Auditor / Treasurer's Office.
Annual Tax Foreclosure Auction
Ward County conducts its annual tax foreclosure property auction on the third Tuesday in November.
Properties offered at the auction have been acquired by Ward County through the tax foreclosure process. The list of properties available for auction is subject to change as properties may be removed prior to the sale.
Current auction information and property listings will be posted below when available.
