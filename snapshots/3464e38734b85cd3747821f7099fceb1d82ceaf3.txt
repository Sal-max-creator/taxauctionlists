Tax Sale Information
The 2026 Tax Sale will be held on Wednesday, September 16th, 2026 in the Multi-Purpose Room &ndash; Basement of the Community Center Building, 317 S. Wayne St., Angola, and that sale will continue until all tracts and real property have been offered for sale.
The Bidding Process: If you are interested in bidding at the tax sale, you may register online at https://sriservices.com/ . If you do not have access to a computer or internet, you may register the morning of the sale. Please arrive the morning of the tax sale at least 30 minutes before the start time so you have enough time to receive your bid number before the start of the sale and confirm your name and address have been entered properly. If registering on line, please bring your registration form and W9 form with you the morning of the tax sale. You will be able to print these forms from the registration web site.
To Redeem your property, you will need to call the Steuben County Auditor's Office to receive the Redemption amount. The Auditor's Office only accepts Certified Funds (Cash, Cashiers Check, or Money Order). The redemption check will need to be made out to: Steuben County Treasurer.
The Redemption period shall expire on Thursday, September 16 th, 2027 for certificates sold in the 2026 Tax Sale.
Tax Sale updates will be posted at www.sriservices.com prior to the tax sale. Please reference their website for the most up to date list.
