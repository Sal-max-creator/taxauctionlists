DELINQUENT TAX SALE
This is a sale of properties that are in their 5th year of delinquency.
Important Notice:
The delinquent tax sale will be held in the middle of May. This year it is May 12, 2027. More information will be on this website in April 12, 2027.
This is a sale of properties that are in their 5th year of delinquency.
The sale is held around the middle of May of the current year. The property listings are available 30 days before the tax sale on the Tax Sale Properties section (see below), as public notices in the legal section in the following newspapers: Ogden Standard Examiner and Davis Journal , and on our website in a power point or Google Slides presentation.
The Public Notice has the following detail on each parcel for sale:
Parcel Number
Address to Parcel (if address is shown only as the "City's Name" this means it is just "raw ground" and doesn't have a mailing address)
Total Acres
Name of Current Property Owner
Amount of Taxes, Penalty, Interest, Cost. (Cost is subject to change if further correspondence is needed)
The 2027 Davis County Delinquent Tax Sale will be held:
May 12th, 2027 @ 10:00 am - Questions 801-451-3329
Pre-registration starts at 9:00 am.
Davis County Administration Building, Room 303, 61 South Main Street, Farmington, UT 84025
Properties may be redeemed up to the time of sale. Once the sale has started (open auction) the properties will be sold to the highest bidder.
Opening bid will include Taxes, Penalties, Interest, and Cost of sale.
The Treasurer's Department will collect the successful bid and the recording fee immediately after the close of the auction.
**Keep in mind that the auction is a "Buyer Beware" sale. If you purchase property you will later be provided with a recorded Tax Deed, which is similar to a Quit Claim Deed. Accordingly, it is your obligation as the purchaser to have researched each property. We hope you have done your research before coming to the sale as all sales are final.**
