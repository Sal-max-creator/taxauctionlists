Online Delinquent Tax Sales
You must carefully read the Comal County Online Tax Sale Rules (PDF) and evaluate these facts considering your anticipated use of the property. Any property may be withdrawn from the sale at any time prior to the sale or during the sale without notification to prospective purchasers.
The last 2026 tax sale is scheduled for November 3rd. The 2027 tax sales are tentatively scheduled for March 2nd, June 1st, August 3rd, and November 2nd.
To bid, you must register on the MVBA website before 5 pm on the day prior to the sale.
For more information, visit the MVBA website or contact MVBA at 830-221-1351.
