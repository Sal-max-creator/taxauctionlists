Tax Foreclosure Sales
Sale at Auction
Sales at public auction are executed by Carteret County's third-party Attorney. Notice of the sale is advertised in two publications (newspapers) and posted on the bulletin board inside the Carteret County Courthouse at least 20 days prior to the auction date. The sale date information displayed on this Web page is updated as it is received from the attorney's office.
Sales are conducted on the steps of the Carteret County Courthouse on the scheduled day of the auction, unless otherwise noted. The successful bidder at this sale is required to make a cash or certified funds deposit of 20% of the bid. To submit a bid, you must be present on the date and time of the sale.
Foreclosure Auction Information

Upset Bids
Successful bids lie open for a 10-day period to allow for the submission of upset bids. The minimum amount accepted for an upset bid is 5% of the original bid amount. Upset bids are made with the Clerk of Superior Court, located on the 2nd Floor of the Carteret County Courthouse. Information regarding bid status is not provided over the telephone.
In the event the successful bidder refuses to accept the deed and honor the bid, the deposit will be forfeited and applied toward defraying costs and expenses. This in no way restricts or limits any other remedies that may be available against a defaulting bidder.
Other Foreclosures
The Clerk of Superior Court&apos;s Special Proceedings Division also maintains a listing of properties scheduled for foreclosure by Deed of Trust holders. These foreclosures are not related to property tax and are not handled by the Carteret County Tax Administration.
Properties in the Foreclosure Process
Each property is being sold as-is, buyer beware, and it is the duty of any bidder to research the property on their own prior to making any bid. The information posted on this site (notice, amount due, property characteristics, structures, pictures, etc.) is for assistance only in the bidder&apos;s research process and cannot and should not be relied upon solely by any bidder.
