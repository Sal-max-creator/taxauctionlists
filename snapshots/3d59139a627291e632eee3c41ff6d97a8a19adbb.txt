Tax Sale Information
Public Sale Date - March 2, 2026
LOCATION & TIME
The public tax sale will take place at 9:00 A.M. at the Sarpy County Boardroom at 1210 Gold Gate Dr, Papillion, NE 68046. Check in will begin and doors will open at 8:30 A.M. Please respect our set-up time of 8:00 A.M. to 8:30 A.M.
Registration Fees &ndash; every entity/person/registrant must pay a non-refundable registration fee of $25 prior to the sale in order to participate.
Bidder Representation &ndash; One person may represent any number of entities/buyers. Random numbers will be assigned.
Tax Sale Fee &ndash; The fee to purchase a tax certificate is $25 per tax certificate and continues to be non-refundable.
Assignment Fee/Tax Deed &ndash; Fees for either are $20 each plus a notary fee, if required.
