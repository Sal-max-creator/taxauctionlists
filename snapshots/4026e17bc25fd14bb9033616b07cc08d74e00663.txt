Property Tax Tax Sale
Tax Sale
April 3, 2024 |
Property Tax |
Bonnie Smith
Notice is hereby given that on May 16, 2024 at 11:00 o’clock A.M., at www.publicsurplus.com Millard County Auditor will offer for sale at public auction and sell to the highest bidder for cash, under the provisions of Section 59-2-1351.1, the real property described on the enclosed Delinquent Tax Billing which located in the county and now delinquent and subject to tax sale. A bid for less than the total amount of taxes, interest, penalty, and administrative costs which are a charge upon such real estate will not be accepted.
Bidder registration is required at www.publicsurplus.com
