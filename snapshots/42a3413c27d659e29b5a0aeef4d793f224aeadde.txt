Delinquent Tax Sale
2026 Sale Location 301 W Market Street Tax Sale - August 24, 2026, 10 am
Real estate that has three years delinquent taxes must be offered for sale to discharge the lien for the delinquent and unpaid taxes. (RSMo 140.160)
List of properties can be found on this website or Advertised in the Star-Journal:
July 24, 2026 - Friday
July 31, 2026 - Friday
August 7, 2026 - Friday
Registration at temporary Courthouse (301 W Market St) the day of sale beginning at 9 am. (No pre-registration)
View Tax sale general information .
View 2026 Tax Sale Information
It is strongly recommended that you consult with an attorney regarding your rights as a purchaser. The Collector and staff of the Collector&apos;s Office cannot provide legal advice. Buying property at tax sale has many restrictions and is very different than foreclosure sales. Please read the attached informaton.
