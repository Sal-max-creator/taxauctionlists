PUBLIC REQUESTS
Residents of Acadia Parish can pay Parish Property Taxes in person at the Acadia Parish Court House, on the second floor, located at 500 N. Parkerson Avenue, Room 215, Crowley, LA 70526.
Please mail payments to PO Box 600, Crowley, LA 70527 .
All card transactions will be charged a 3.45% fee or a $3.95 minimum
For your convenience, you can now pay your p arish property taxes online.
Please visit : https://snstaxpayments.com/acadia
All payments, links, pages, etc. associated with the Acadia Parish Sheriff's Office are secure. If you are encountered with an unsecure page, please advance through it, as your information is safe.
DELINQUENT PROPERTY TAX LIEN AUCTION
The annual Parish online delinquent property tax lien auction is set for Wednesday, June 24th, 2026 beginning at 11am with the sale ending at 2pm. Tax lien auction registration will be available starting 8am Monday, May 25th, 2026. The registration process will end at noon Thursday, June 18th, 2026. To register for the tax sale, the following link can be used: TAX LEIN AUCTION ONLINE or you may contact our office at 337-788-8750 for any questions. Only form of payment accepted will be ACH Withdrawal. If you have issues during registration, please contact Zeus Auction at 800-800-9588.
The tax lien auction advertisement will run in the local paper, The Crowley Post Signal, Wednesday, June 10th, 2026. Viewing of the listing will be available on our website that day.
Payments for delinquent taxes will be accepted until 10AM on Wednesday, June 24th, 2026.
May 1st, 2026 through June 24th, 2026, Cash, Money Order, Certified Check or online payments will be the only forms of payment accepted for delinquent taxes.
If you need further assistance, please call 337.788.8750 to speak with a Deputy in the Tax Department.
