Tax Sale Information
Date and Time
First Tuesday of every month at 10:00 a.m.
Location
Auctions are held on-line, sign up here: https://ellis.texas.sheriffsaleauctions.com/
To Obtain a Tax Sale List
Tax Sale Properties | Linebarger Goggan Blair & Sampson, LLP (lgbs.com) .
Payment
Payment in full is required of the winning bidder on the day of sale.
Other Costs
Taxes for post judgment years may also be due from the buyer.
