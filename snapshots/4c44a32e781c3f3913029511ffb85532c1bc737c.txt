Previous Delinquent Tax Sale: March 30th, 2026
The tax sale will be held on Monday, March 30th, 2026 at 1:30 p.m . in the County Extension Office of Anderson County located at 411 S Oak St in Garnett Kansas. Payment can be made in the form of cash or check. Please note that anyone with unpaid taxes in Anderson County will not be eligible to bid at the tax sale. Registration for the sale is in person on the day of the sale.
