Public Tax Sale
Public Delinquent Tax Sale List Is Available Tuesday Before Tax Sale
Investor Information
Public Tax Sale Registration Form
Fillable W-9 Form
Tax Sale Investors must understand the process of purchasing tax sale certificates, before the tax sale. There are risks involved. When purchasing a certificate you are purchasing delinquent taxes, not the property. You must hold that certificate and pay subsequent taxes each year as they become delinquent for three years. If the homeowner does not redeem that certificate by the end of three years, you will have nine months to foreclose on the property. It is highly advised to seek legal counsel at the maturity of your certificates. The Otoe County Treasurer's office WILL NOT provide legal advice.
Sale Information:
Advertising:
The most current Delinquent Tax List is available online the Tuesday before the tax sale.
Registration Fee:
A $25.00 registration fee must accompany an Otoe County Tax Sale Registration Form.
Registrations and Registration Fees Must Be Received by the Treasurer's Office No Later Than the Thursday before the First Monday in March.
One bidder can represent up to 5 (five) investors.
Sale Procedure:
Tax Sale Investors agree to buy the liens for the delinquent tax amounts interest and advertising plus any other delinquent tax assessed to each parcel. Some parcels may have multiple years.&#8239; NE Statute 77-1807 .
The Otoe County Tax Sale will be conducted in Round Robin format. Numbers are assigned after the buyers have signed in. The number assigned shall represent the order in which each "buyer" will sit. "Buers" will be offered a parcel, they may purchase the parcel offered or pass. Should they pass, the parcel will be offered to the next "buyer" until that parcel is purchased, continuing with each parcel until all parcels are sold. Parcels will be offered in high dollar to low dollar order, starting with the highest dollar parcel at the time of the sale. Should a "buyer" pass on the parcel offered, they will not have an opportunity to purchase another parcel until their next turn.
Payment:
Company check, cash or personal check can be used for registration fee and certificates purchased. A $25.00 fee is charged for each certificate sold. There will be an additional $25.00 fee for any re-assignment of certificates. Re-assignments will not be done day of sale. We will hold your certificates in our office for safekeeping and send payment when redeemed. This ensures a faster turnaround of your funds. If the taxes on a parcel are paid before the certificate is issued, there are no fees charged to the investor for that parcel.
Legal Advice:
Nebraska statutes&#8239; 77-1801 thru 77-1941 &#8239;covers all aspects of buying and foreclosing on tax sale certificates. You must consult with an attorney for any legal advice. The Otoe County Treasurers office&#8239; WILL NOT &#8239;provide any guidance or legal advice pertaining to tax sale certificates.
Other Tax Sale Information
Delinquent taxes are advertised for three consecutive weeks in February
The Public Sale is the first Monday in March at 9:00 a.m.
The Public Sale closes after all parcels are offered for sale.
Interest rate earned is 14%
During the life of the certificate, subsequent taxes must be paid as they become delinquent.
You are responsible for your own foreclosure process when certificates mature.
Private, public, and county tax sale certificates may be assigned.&#8239;
We do not allow purchases via fax or telephone.
The purchaser must research their own information to purchase.
If paying by check, please provide a Blank Check, the total amount of your purchase will be filled in and a copy will be emailed to you.
Tax amounts do not include interest. Interest will be figured to the date the certificate is taken out.
A property owner has three years (from the date of the certificate's issuance) to redeem the tax sale certificate before foreclosure can begin.
The holder of a tax sale certificate has no ownership of the property,&#8239; only a lien.
