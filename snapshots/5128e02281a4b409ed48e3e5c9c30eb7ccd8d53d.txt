Tax Sale Date
Notices
Tax Sales are scheduled on the first Tuesday of any given month on the courthouse steps at 12 noon.
Notice of the sale is published once a week for the four weeks immediately preceding the sale in the legal organ (newspaper) for the county, which is presently The Tribune & Georgian .
