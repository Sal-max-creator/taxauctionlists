Delinquent Taxes
Overview
In Anderson County, suits filed by the counties and cities to collect delinquent taxes are filed in Chancery Court.
Upon the filing of the lawsuit, the Clerk and Master collects the delinquent taxes, plus statutory interest and penalty, court costs and attorney fees.
