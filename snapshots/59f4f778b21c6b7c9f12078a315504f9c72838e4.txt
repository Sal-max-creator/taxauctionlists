2027 Tax Sale Notice
The Anderson County Tax Sale will be held on Monday, October 25, 2027 at the Civic Center of Anderson, located at 3027 Martin Luther King Jr. Blvd, Anderson, SC 29625. Beginning September 30 , 2027 , information concerning online bidder registration, property listings and property maps will be available on this site.
