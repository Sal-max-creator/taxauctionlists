Tax Foreclosure Auctions
Every year, properties that have been foreclosed due to delinquent property taxes are auctioned in a public sale. Sales from previous years are recorded in the files below.
Learn more about tax sale auctions on the site that manages the auctions each year, www.tax-sale.info
Contact the Treasurer's Office to learn more about these auctions, or visit the Property Tax Assistance page to learn about foreclosure prevention options.
