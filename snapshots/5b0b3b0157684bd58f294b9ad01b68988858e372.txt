gaps and gores, deficiency in quantity, all questions of boundaries, location and acreage, which a
current and accurate survey would disclose, roadway, environmental and wetlands matters,
unrecorded easements, matters which would be disclosed by an inspection of the premises, or any
other matter of record or not of record. The same shall be subject to confirmation by the Treasurer of
