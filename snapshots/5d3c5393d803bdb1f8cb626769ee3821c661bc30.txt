Tax Sale
The Hancock County Tax Sale will be held online . The internet sale will be held Friday , September 18th, 2026, starting at 10 am . Parcels will be closing in batches. Batches are groups of items with the same closing time. The properties that do not sell the first time through the sale will be offered again in a &apos;last batch&apos; to give interested buyers one last opportunity to bid.
Payment requirements will be cash, cashier&apos;s check, certified funds, or wire transfer. Payment must be received by Tues day , September 22, 2026, at noon . The sale will be conducted by SRI Inc. To register for this sale, you will need to become a member and then register for the Hancock County sale specifically. For more information regarding the auction system please call 800-800-9588. It is the purchaser&apos;s responsibility to understand what they are bidding on and the requirements after the tax sale in order to be able to receive a tax deed on the property.
For information on properties eligible for Sheriff&apos;s sale or foreclosure sale contact the Hancock County Sheriff&apos;s Department at 317-477-1147.
Hancock County holds an annual tax sale and tax certificate sale to sell liens against properties with delinquent property taxes.
It is the responsibility of the purchaser to follow Indiana tax sale and redemption law which can be found in Indiana Code 6-1.1-24 and 6-1.1-25.
No personal checks accepted.
Online auctions are held at Zeusauction.com .
