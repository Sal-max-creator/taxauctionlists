A Friendly Reminder:
The end of the redemption period for properties sold at the 2025 Tax Sale is approaching. If you had a property that was sold in that tax sale, the last day to redeem it is Wednesday, November 4, 2026, by 5 PM. Please stop by our office to take care of this ASAP.
The Lancaster County Delinquent Tax Collectors Office is responsible for the collection of taxes that are delinquent for real estate, mobile homes, business personal property and equipment, boats, motors, airplanes, and campers. These taxes are delinquent after March 16th each year.
No personal checks will be accepted.
Cash, debit / credit card, cashier&apos;s checks or money orders accepted.
Please contact the Delinquent Tax Office with any questions.
If you receive any documentation/notification concerning the sale of any property for Delinquent Taxes outside of the Lancaster County Tax Collector, please contact our office at 803-283-8885.

2026 TAX Sale Advertisement - Week 2
ATTENTION:
DELINQUENT TAX SALE
STATE OF SOUTH CAROLINA, COUNTY OF LANCASTER
By virtue of Tax Execution, the Delinquent Tax Collector for Lancaster County will sell to the highest bidder on sales day, November 9, 2026, at 9 am at the Springdale Recreation Center, 260 S Plantation Road, Lancaster, South Carolina. The following described properties to satisfy unpaid taxes to the county of Lancaster and all penalties and costs thereon as provided in section 12-51-40 of the code of laws of South Carolina, 1976 as amended. No personal checks accepted .
Bidders may register online at THIS LINK with a $25 Fee September 8, 2026, through November 5, 2026. Any bidder wishing to register November 6, 2026, MUST apply in person or via phone call as the online link will be closed November 5, 2026.
Registration may be closed early subject to capacity of facility being reached. There will be NO Registration on the day of the tax sale. Please contact the Delinquent Tax Collector's Office for more information. 101 N Main Street, Lancaster, SC 29720 803-283-8885.
The last day of redemption for properties that were sold at the tax sale on November 3, 2025, is November 4, 2026. The delinquent tax office will be closed on November 9, 2026, to conduct the 2026 tax sale.
BIDDER REGISTRATION FOR THE 2026 TAX SALE WILL BEGIN ONLINE SEPTEMBER 8, 2026. THERE IS A NONREFUNDABLE BIDDER REGISTRATION FEE OF $25 DUE AT TIME OF REGISTRATION. YOU MAY ALSO STOP BY OUR OFFICE TO REGISTER.
