Tax Lien Auction
The 2026 Blount County Tax Lien Auction and Sale will be held online on Tuesday, March 24, 2026, at 9:00 am CST.
Please click the following link for the current list of parcels with an outstanding tax amount: https://altags.com/blount_license/Delinquent.aspx
The link below provides information regarding the law as it relates to tax lien sales, specifically under the 2025 Code of Alabama, Title 40 Revenue and Taxation, Chapter 10 Sale of Land, Article 7 Sale of Tax Liens:
https://law.justia.com/codes/alabama/title-40/chapter-10/article-7
While our office manages the auction process and Ingenuity hosts it, neither they nor we can provide personal legal advice. We strongly recommend consulting a competent attorney for specific legal guidance on purchasing tax-delinquent property.
Online Tax Lien Auction Info
Our Tax Lien Auction will be hosted by Ingenuity Auctions and will take place online at Ingenuity Auctions .
To activate your bidder account and begin exploring auctions, please create an account and then complete your registration here .
Registration should only take 2-3 minutes. You'll verify your email, create a secure password, and provide any additional details required for bidding. Once registered, you'll be able to:
Browse and bid on live and upcoming tax auctions
View and Claim your First Rights
Receive bid notifications and win alerts
If you have any questions while registering or need help getting started, our support team is here to assist you. Contact us at support@ingenuityauctions.com or call (205) 263-4560.
