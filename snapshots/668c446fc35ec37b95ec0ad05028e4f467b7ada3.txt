Hamilton County Tax Sale
For general information about county property tax sale, please visit the SRI website .
To be removed from a Tax Sale, payment in full should be made to the Hamilton County Treasurer&apos;s Office no later than noon on the day before the tax sale. Funds must be certified (cash, cashier checks and money orders) and must be made payable to the Hamilton County Treasurer.
Important Note
The owner must file a claim for the surplus funds within 3 years after the date of the tax sale in which the parcel was sold.
