GENERAL INFORMATION FOR ALL LAND AUCTIONS
Please contact the Pine County Land Department at 320-216-4226 if you have questions after viewing the following information.
All tracts are sold "as-is" and must be purchased at or above the indicated value for that tract (EMV, appraised value, minimum bid, etc).
Contract for deed will be a payment option for some tracts, whereas payment in full will be required for other tracts. This is determined based on date of forfeiture and state statute. See terms and conditions of individual land auctions.
Interest Rates on Contract For Deed Purchases
Potential buyers are encouraged to thoroughly research properties and check with local planning and zoning authorities.
Information on searching land records (Landshark) can be found on the Pine County Recorder Office website .
Parcels can also be searched for on the Pine County Beacon website .
Official plats with lot dimensions and other details can be found here , and may have useful information on some of the land auction tracts.
Landlocked Property Info:
Landlocked land does not have legal access. We do not recommend anyone purchase landlocked land without legal access.
However, for those interested in landlocked land, you can contact and arrange access with adjacent land owners. If cooperative access cannot be obtained, please be aware that Minnesota State Statute 164.08 offers specific direction on gaining access through cartway law. All cartway proceedings are unique to the situation. Be aware that typical cartway process may involve legal expenses, purchase of the cartway and/or other fees paid to the land owner, survey costs, construction costs, etc.
The local township is actively involved in the cartway process.
