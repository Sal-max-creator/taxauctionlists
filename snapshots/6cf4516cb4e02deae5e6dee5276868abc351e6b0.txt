Delinquent Property Tax List to Appear in St. Tammany Farmer
In anticipation of its annual tax lien auction on July 29, the St. Tammany Parish Sheriff's Office will advertise the list of delinquent properties in the July 15th edition of The St. Tammany Farmer.
The list will also be posted under "Public Notices" on the St. Tammany Parish Sheriff's Office website.
In accordance with L.R.S. 47:2153, this is the public notice of properties still delinquent for the 2025 tax year. As of current, there are approximately 2,000 properties with taxes and/or related fees due to the parish and the municipalities of Slidell, Mandeville, Covington, Pearl River, Abita Springs and Madisonville. The list on the STPSO website will be updated more frequently.
Property tax bills for 2025 were mailed Nov. 11, 2025. Delinquent notices were sent on February 23, 2026.
The STPSO has contracted with SRI, Inc. to utilize its online tax lien auction platform. The auction will take place at www.zeusauction.com , beginning at 8 a.m. and closing at 4 p.m. on July 29th.
Those interested in participating in the auction will be required to create an account on the website and register for the St. Tammany Parish auction. Registration will open on July 1st at 8 a.m. and close July 22nd at 12 p.m. Registrants will have access to a Louisiana specific practice auction to become familiar with the features of the program. For more information with this process, SRI can be reached at (800) 800-9588. The tax lien auction laws were significantly changed starting January 1, 2026. It is imperative that any party interested in partaking in the process to read these new laws before participating. It is important that properties are given due diligence as STPSO is not responsible for erroneous listings.
Delinquent taxpayers are strongly encouraged to make their payments by noon on July 28 so payments can be recorded, and those properties removed from the website before the auction begins.
The tax lien auction process completes the annual property tax collection cycle. The tax lien auction is not for the structure or land itself, but the delinquent monetary obligation of taxes, fees, and the percentage of interest that is to be collected. This places a lien and privilege against the delinquent property. A tax lien auction party pays the tax due, as well as interest and other costs incurred in the collection process.
Owners of property that sell at the tax lien auction have a three-year period during which they can extinguish the tax lien certificate with the STPSO by paying the required payoff amount. The amounts collected are then used to reimburse the tax lien certificate holder.
For additional information on taxes owed or the tax lien auction process, call the St. Tammany Parish Sheriff's Office Property Tax Department at (985) 809-8217.
