Sheriff&#8217;s Sale Court Order Updates
UPDATE 02/04/22 &#8211; Order of Court: Continuance of Sheriff Sale Proceedings for Certain Residential Real Estate
Before you continue, please read the court order from August 2020 regarding virtual sheriff&#8217;s sales. If you would like to participate in the virtual sale, you must register. A member of the Sheriff&#8217;s Office will contact you via email and participants shall provide a copy of their valid state ID or Driver&#8217;s License to obtain a bidder number. Failure to do so will result in no admittance to virtual sale. Please fill out ALL fields on the Bidder Registration form . Failure to do so will result in an incomplete submittal and you will not receive a confirmation email or bidder number. Upon submittal, please allow two business days to receive your bidder number, as this process is not automatic.
