Tax Deeds
The Tax Deeds Section of the Official Records Division manages the tax deed sales for the Lake County Clerk of the Circuit Court & Comptroller.
Tax Certificates are sold annually by the Lake County Tax Collector’s Office for properties that have delinquent taxes. After a minimum of two years have elapsed, and upon request of the certificate holder for a tax deed sale, the certificate is forwarded to the Clerk’s Office, and a tax deed sale is held. The highest bidder will be issued a tax deed on the property.
Tax deed sales are administered by the Clerk’s Office online.
For additional information on tax deed sales, review the Frequently Asked Questions section of the website.
