5 Year Delinquent Tax Sale
The 5 Year Delinquent Tax Sale occurs when the county sells parcels of land that are five years delinquent in the payment of property taxes due. Sanpete County Ordinance, 03-15-2022 (PDF) , establishes the method, rules, and procedures for conducting the annual tax sale.
The 2026 Delinquent Tax Sale will be held on May 21, 2026, at 10 am on the Public Surplus Website only . Bidders are required to register before the sale at the Public Surplus Website .
2026 List of Delinquent Properties (PDF) - List will be updated as properties are redeemed. (List will be published 4 weeks before the sale)
A property may be redeemed in behalf of the record owner by any person, at any time before the start of the sale, on the scheduled date. Property is redeemed by paying to the Sanpete County Treasurer all delinquent taxes, interest, penalties, and administrative costs that have accrued on the property. Paying the delinquent amount does not grant ownership to the individual(s) or party(s) paying all delinquent taxes, interest, penalties, and administrative costs. This only removes the property from the sale.
Questions can be directed to the Sanpete County Auditor via email call or by calling 435-835-2142.
