Delinquent Property Tax Information
Property within Morton County is subject to foreclosure in the event that the property taxes, specials, and any interest/penalties become delinquent in the third year. Morton County Auditor&#8217;s office accrues any delinquent property, notifies all interested parties of the terms and conditions, and conducts an annual sale of any property with an unsatisfied tax debt.
June 1 &#8211; Foreclosure notice will be given to the property owner, occupants of the property, and any other person entitled to possession of or interest in the property before June 1.
August 1 &#8211; A notice of delinquent property will be published in the official county newspaper before August 1.
October 1 &#8211; Tax Lien foreclosure date. Taxes, Specials, and any Interest/Penalties for delinquent years must be paid in full to the Morton County Treasurers office before 5 p.m.
October 2 , until the sale on the 3rd Tuesday in November &#8211; after October 1, the owner must bring ALL outstanding Taxes, Specials, and any Interest/Penalties current. Payment may be made to the Morton County Treasurer&#8217;s Office before the scheduled start time of the sale on the 3rd Tuesday of November.
At Least 15 days prior to annual sale . &#8211; A notice of properties for sale will be posted in the Auditor&#8217;s Office and the county website.
At Least 10 days prior to annual sale. &#8211; A notice of properties for sale will be posted in the official county newspaper.
3rd Tuesday of November &#8211; Annual sale is held
