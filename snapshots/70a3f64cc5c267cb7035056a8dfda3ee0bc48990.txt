OVERVIEW
What we CAN help you with&hellip;
Paying Real Estate and Mobile Home Taxes
Obtaining Tax clearance for Mobile Homes
Releasing MOBILE HOME liens
Answering any questions about payments for any Property or Mobile Home Taxes
Paying REAL ESTATE or MOBILE HOME property taxes online or by phone
We CANNOT help you with&hellip;
Changing your mailing address on Property OR Mobile Home Tax bills &ndash; contact the Assessor's Office 618-244-8016
Real Estate Tax Exemptions (Homestead, Disability, Senior Citizen Assessment Freeze) &ndash; contact the Assessor's Office 618-244-8016
Complaints about the AMOUNT of your Real Estate Taxes - contact the taxing districts listed on your tax bill
Delinquent tax redemption - contact the County Clerk 618-244-8020
