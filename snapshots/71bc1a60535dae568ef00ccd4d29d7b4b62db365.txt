Public Notice of Annual Ewing Township Tax Sale
December 2, 2025
The Township of Ewing announces the sale of 2025 and prior year delinquent taxes, sewer, and other municipal charges through an online auction on December 30, 2025.
For a listing of all parcels, delinquencies, and costs, please visit https://ewing.newjerseytaxsale.com/ .
See also the list in PDF format at https://ewingnj.org/filelink/fileman-files/Tax%20Collector/2025-11-30-TAX-SALE-LIST-ADVERTISING.pdf .
For additional information about the Ewing Tax Sale please check out our Tax Sale Information webpage.
For additional information about NJ Tax Sales check out this publication from the Department of Community Affairs.
