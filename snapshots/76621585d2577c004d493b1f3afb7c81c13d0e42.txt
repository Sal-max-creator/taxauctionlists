Tax Forfeiture
In collaboration with the Wright County GIS department, a Tax Forfeited Properties GIS tool has been provided to the public. This mapping tool shows all available tax forfeited properties within Wright highlighted in "Green". All sold tax forfeited properties in "Red". Available tax forfeited properties can only be purchased during our over-the-counter sales or during our online auctions via Public Surplus.

Notice of Surplus Proceeds from Tax-Forfeited Land Sales. An interested party may submit a claim for the surplus proceeds by filing a claim with the Wright County auditor. For a list of Tax-Forfeited parcel sales that had surplus proceeds, Click Here.
Tax forfeited parcels are properties on which real estate taxes were not paid. Title to the land and buildings were forfeited, and they are now vested in the State of Minnesota. Wright County manages the properties while in trust for the State.
All property is sold &apos;as is&apos; and may not conform to local building and zoning ordinances. All sales are final and no refunds or exchanges are permitted.
We highly recommend that you thoroughly research any tax forfeited parcels before purchasing. Most liens and mortgages, except Federal and State Tax Liens, are canceled at the time of forfeiture and it is the responsibility of the purchaser to verify this information. Any prior special assessments may be reassessed by the city/townships once sold. For specific legal issues you may want to consult an attorney.
Wright County Finance & Taxpayer Services shall charge and collect, in full at the time of sale for the State of Minnesota, an assurance fee of 3% of the total sale price of the property. Additional fees such as recording fees, deed fees/taxes, and third-party online auction fees will be explained at the time of payment.
