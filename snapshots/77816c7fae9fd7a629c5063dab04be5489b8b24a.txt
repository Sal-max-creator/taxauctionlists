Tax Sale Information
Each year, the County Clerk&#8217;s Office is responsible for conducting a tax sale on any remaining delinquent tax bills. During that time, the delinquent tax bills are eligible to be purchased by a Third Party.
2025 DELINQUENT TAX SALE: TUESDAY, AUGUST 4, 2026 @ 9:00AM
BOONE COUNTY 2025 DELINQUENT TAX BILL SALE DETAILS
Delinquent Tax Lottery Sale Date: Tuesday, August 4, 2026 @ 9:00am
Burlington Office – Real Estate Department
2950 Washington Square
Burlington, KY 41005
The following information is being provided to answer questions you may have pertaining to the structure and implementation of our upcoming tax sale. If you have any questions remaining after reviewing this information, please do not hesitate to contact us.
Registration: All Third-Party Purchasers MUST register with the Boone County Clerk’s office before participating in the tax sale. Registration is July 21–July 28, 2026. All fees (registration & filing) will be collected at the time of registration via certified funds.
Priority Purchases: All Third-Party Purchasers having priority on any current year certificates of delinquency must submit a list of priority bills separate from lottery lists and 100% of the August pay off amount for the bill.
Deposit: In order to participate in our sale and in compliance with 103 KAR 5:180 S4(c) each Third-Party Purchaser will be required to provide twenty-five (25) percent of the value of the purchaser’s list via certified funds no later than July 29, 2025 .
Draw: W e will draw for the order of placement via online link provided once you register. The drawing will take place Friday, July 31, 2026.
List: We have updated our spreadsheets for submitting your priority and lottery lists. Please use the spreadsheet provided.
PLEASE REMEMBER: The master list includes ALL delinquencies; we ask that you please research and are aware of what you agree to purchase, as we cannot guarantee the waiver or refund.
