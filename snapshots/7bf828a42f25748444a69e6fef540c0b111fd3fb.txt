Tax Sale Date
Anyone wanting to purchase more than 3 tax bills in any one county or more than 5 bills in the state must register with the state www.revenue.ky.gov at least 60 days prior to the tax sale.
The Christian County Clerk Delinquent Property Tax Sale date is July 28, 2026 , beginning at 9:00 AM Central Daylight Time. All interested participants must register with the County Clerk's office by close of business on July 20, 2026 . If you have any questions, please call (270) 887-4109.
