Tax Foreclosure
Tax Foreclosure is a section in Treasury & Budget that protects the interests of property tax payers (those who pay timely). Protection is achieved by pursuing delinquent tax accounts throughout a three year process. Fairbanks North Star Borough holds sales of properties acquired through tax foreclosure at least once every two years.
UPCOMING IMPORTANT DATES with ADDITIONAL FEES TO BE ADDED (if applicable):

Paying Delinquent Property Taxes
At this time, to redeem a property set to be in the upcoming Tax Foreclose Sale, all property taxes must be paid current (through 2026).
ALL payments must be paid by GUARANTEED FUNDS.
Cashier&apos;s Checks and Money Orders must be made out to " FNSB ". Be sure to include your Parcel Account Number ( PAN ) in the memo field. If a wire transfer would be easier, please contact our office for more information.
Keep in mind, to keep a property out of the Tax Foreclosure Sale, funds MUST be received by our office no later than 5:00 P.M. AKDT September 10,2027 .
Online payments and pay by phone options are no longer available.
Contact Us:
Prior to making any payments, please contact our office for an accurate payoff amount.
