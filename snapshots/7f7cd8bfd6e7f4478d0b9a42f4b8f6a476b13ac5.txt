COUNTY-OWNED PROPERTY SALE
Purpose
To sell property owned by Delta County that are not being retained or leased in accordance with C.R.S. 39-11-143 (2.5) or (3).
When and Where
The County owned property sale will be held annually by SRI, Inc. through the Zeus Auction Program . Items available for bid may be viewed at the Zeus Auction website if a sale is scheduled.
Registration
Registration information will be posted here if a sale is scheduled. NO LATE REGISTRATIONS WILL BE ALLOWED.
See the Rules for County-Owned Property Sale for more information.
Elements of Risk
Neither the auctioneer nor the seller makes any warranties or representations of any kind or nature with respect to said property. All sales are final. Descriptions are for simple identification purposes only, no representations are made to the authenticity, age, origin or value. Buyer relies solely on his/her own judgment when bidding. All sales are final. It is the bidders&apos; responsibility to know what they are bidding on.

ANNUAL TAX LIEN SALE
Purpose
The Treasurer is required by law to collect property taxes and distribute them to the county&apos;s taxing authorities. The tax lien sale is held to collect unpaid taxes so that the taxing authorities can meet their budgets. These taxes are purchased by investors who earn interest on the tax liens placed on the property. The interest rate on the investment is set at 9 points above the Federal Reserve's discount rate on September 1st each year.
When and Where
The annual tax lien sale will be held online on the first Thursday in November, beginning at 8:00 a.m. MST on the Zeus Auction website. For sale information and rules, see the Rules for Annual Tax Lien Sale page.
2026 Tax Lien Sale: Important Dates
Registration: Friday, October 23, through Friday, October 30, 2026. Late registrations will not be accepted.
Online sale: Thursday, November 5, 2026, beginning at 8 a.m. Mountain Time.
Register and participate through the Zeus Auction website . Registration is required for the Delta County auction.
Read the annual tax lien sale rules and registration requirements (PDF)
Questions? Contact the Treasurer's Office at 970-874-2135 or treasurer@deltacountyco.gov .
By statute, C.R.S. 39-11-151 (1)(b), no tax lien shall be sold to an elected or county official, to a county employee, or to a member of the immediate family of such person or to the agent of any such county official or employee during the time the official or employee holds office or is employed.
Elements of Risk
There is an element of risk in buying tax liens. All liens are sold buyer beware.

FAQs
Can I receive my property tax statement electronically?
Yes! You can create an account at https://enoticesonline.com using the unique code found on your most recent property tax statement. If you do not have this code, please contact the Treasurer's Office for assistance.

How are taxes determined?
Property taxes are figured by multiplying the assessed value of your property (as determined by the assessor's office) by the mill levy which is set by the various taxing entities. The Treasurer's Office collects these taxes and distributes the monies to the taxing entities. Remember: Taxes due are for the previous year.

Payment – When? Where? How?
You have the option to pay your property taxes either in full or in two equal installments. If you choose to pay in full, the payment is due by April 30. If you opt for two installments, the first payment is due by the end of February, and the second payment is due by June 15.
Payments can be made in person at the Treasurer's Office, located at 501 Palmer Street, Suite 202, Delta, or at the County Annex Building, 196 W. Hotchkiss Avenue, Hotchkiss. You may also mail your payment to: Delta County Treasurer, PO Box 580, Grand Junction, CO 81502-0580. Please note that only a United States Postal Service postmark will be accepted as proof of meeting payment deadlines.
We accept cash, checks, money orders, cashier's checks, or certified funds. You can also pay by credit card or e-check through GovPros or by calling (877) 377-1027.
