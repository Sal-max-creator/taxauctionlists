Tax Collector
The Office of the Tax Collector is responsible for the billing, collection, reporting and enforcement of over 30,000 individual tax accounts and a similar number of sewer usage accounts. Amounts charged and payments made are all individually maintained. In addition, with centralized receipting, this office is responsible for the reporting and depositing of all funds collected by the various departments within the municipality.
The Office of the Tax Collector is located at: 100 Municipal Blvd., Edison NJ 08817 (On the 2nd floor of the Municipal Building). The office hours are 8 am to 4:30 pm. If you need to contact us, we can be reached by email at TaxCollector@EdisonNJ.gov or by phone (732) 248-7228. We are here to help. Click on the links below.
