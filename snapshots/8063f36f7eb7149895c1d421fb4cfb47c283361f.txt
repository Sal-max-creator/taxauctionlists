To be a bidder, you must be a Missouri State Resident and you will be the one purchasing any property in the Tax Sale and your name will appear on the Certificate of Purchase and Collector's Deed.
If you are purchasing on behalf of a company, corporation, lending institution or other organization, you must provide proper documentation authorizing you to act on their behalf.
After purchasing a property, if you want to transfer ownership you will need to fill out the middle section of the Recorded Certificate of Purchase* and sign it in front of a Notary.
Transferring of ownership does not apply to a purchase made on behalf of a non-resident.
*Recorded Certificate of Purchase is retained by the Collector's Office. You must contact our office to make a change of ownership on the Certificate of Purchase. The Certificate of Purchase that you receive in September, printed on blue paper, is for your records only.
Bidder Registration Process
Complete the Register as a New Bidder Form below.
Verify the Registration Information is correct and accurate.
Print the Tax Sale Bidder Documents downloaded after registration.
Sign the Tax Sale Bidder Certification and Bidder Sheet
On Tax Sale Day
Bring all of the downloaded and signed Tax Sale Documents with you to the Courthouse.
Bring your Missouri State Issued Identification to prove Residency and Identity.
If acting as an agent for a Company or Out-of-State individual, bring documentation of relationship.
Arrive at Courthouse by 8:30 am on Monday, August 23, 2027.
Register in Collector's Office prior to Tax Sale.
