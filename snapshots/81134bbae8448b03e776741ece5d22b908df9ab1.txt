ADA COUNTY TAX DEED PROCESS
OVERVIEW
3-year delinquency: Each January 1, real property with a 3- year tax delinquency may be considered for tax deed.
Notice: The record owner(s) of the real property and parties in interest are notified of the pending issue of tax deed per Idaho Code Title 63, Chapter 10.
Partial payments: Accepted before the hearing but do not stop the tax deed process.
KEY STEPS
Notice of Pending Issue of Tax Deed: Sent by certified mail 2-5 months before the hearing.
Publication of Notice: May be published weekly for 4 consecutive weeks before the hearing.
Affidavit of Compliance: States facts constituting compliance with the issuance of notice of pending tax deed and recorded no less than 5 working days before the hearing.
Hearing: Open to the public and entered into the county minutes.
Evidence: The record owner(s) and parties in interest may provide evidence and testify.
Issuance of tax deed: Required by law to be recorded in favor of the county, if the statutory requirements are satisfied.
Redemption: Before the property is sold or transferred by the county, the record owner(s) or any party in interest may redeem by paying all taxes (delinquent and current), late charges, interest and costs.
PUBLIC AUCTION & EXCESS PROCEEDS
Public Auction: Held within 14 months from the issuance of the tax deed.
Minimum bid: Includes the total of all delinquency taxes, late charges, interest and costs.
Excess Proceeds: The winning bid amount, less taxes, late charges, interest and costs.
Notice of Excess Proceeds: Mailed within 30 days of the auction.
Claim to Excess Proceeds: Parties in interest must make a claim on the proceeds within 60 days of receiving the notice.
Payment of Excess Proceeds: County must pay valid claims, in priority of lien, to parties in interest within 60 days.
Return of Excess Proceeds to Prior Owner: All funds after payment to parties in interest are returned to the record owner(s) of the property at the time the tax deed was issued.
