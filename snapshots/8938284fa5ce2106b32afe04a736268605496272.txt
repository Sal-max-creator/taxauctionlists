NOTICE TO TAXPAYERS
If your property is eligible for tax sale and you'd like to remove it, we encourage you to contact our office as soon as possible to discuss the options available to you. Every case is different, so it's in your best interest to speak with a member of our team to be provided with the most accurate information.
2026 Upset Tax Sale
PLEASE READ BELOW AND SEE BID4ASSETS.COM FOR ADDITIONAL
INFORMATION!
Registration will be open from August 3, 2026 to
August 28, 2026 .
There is a multi-step registration process required in order
to participate in one of our sales. Please follow the steps below to ensure you
are fully able to participate.
Register with Bid4Assets, if you do not already have an active profile.
Complete the "Vesting"/"Funding" sections of Bid4Assets to provide
all of your details, be provided with a copy of the conditions, e-sign the affidavit, and upload your documents - ID, business documents, etc.
Bid4Assets will review
your information and follow up with you if there are any issues with what
you've uploaded.
If your registration with them is complete and accurate, they will provide you with a link to
set up a virtual or in-person meeting with our office.
Once you have registered
with Bid4Assets, had your meeting with Tax Claim and funded your deposit, you are completely registered and authorized to participate in the sale.
ABSOLUTE LAST DAY TO HAVE ALL REGISTRATION STEPS
COMPLETED WITH BID4ASSETS IS AUGUST 28, 2026. NO EXCEPTIONS.
Important reminders to consider:
Appointments are first come, first served
Appointments will be canceled if not all of the steps were followed appropriately.
We will not contact you
to schedule an appointment.
If you fail to appear for your appointment,
it will be your responsibility to reschedule, if slots are still
available.
If you fail to appear for two appointments, you will be prohibited from registering for this sale.
Be advised, if you fail
to comply with one or more of these steps you WILL NOT be able to
participate in this sale.
