Options For Certified Properties
If you are facing the possibility of a Tax or Certificate Sale, or your property has been sold at Tax or Certificate Sale, you may be able to redeem your property by paying all taxes dues, additional fees and daily accruing interest. General deadlines for redemptions are listed below.
Before the Tax Sale
To be removed from Tax Sale, all delinquent taxes, penalties and fees must be paid with certified funds to the Porter County Treasurer prior to the Tax Sale. Please refer to your Notice of Tax Sale for details.
Taxpayers may qualify to enter into a Tax Sale Payment Plan with the Treasurer to avoid having their property sold at Tax Sale. For questions about removing your property from a Tax Sale, or details and qualifications for a Tax Sale Payment Plan, EMAIL the Treasurer's office or call (219) 465-3470
If Sold At Tax Sale
If your property is sold at a Tax Sale, redemption must be completed within one year of the Tax Sale date. For more information or to redeem a property, please EMAIL the Auditor’s office or call (219) 465-3449.
If Not Sold At Tax Sale & County Intends To Pursue Title
If your property is not sold at Tax Sale and the County intends to pursue title, redemption must be completed with 120 days of the Tax Sale date. For more information or to redeem a property, please EMAIL the Auditor’s office or call (219) 465-3449.
If Sold At Certificate Sale
If you property is not sold at Tax Sale and the County does not pursue title, your property will then be subject to a Certificate Sale. Redemption must be completed with 120 Days of the Certificate Sale date. For more information or to redeem a property, please EMAIL the Auditor’s office or call (219) 465-3449.
