An informational packet about the 2026 tax sale is available for purchase at the Tax Collector&apos;s Office at City Hall for $25.
This packet explains the tax sale process and the bidding process in detail, and provides answers to questions potential bidders most frequently ask about tax sales. It also contains a list of the properties offered for sale, and copies of the relevant Connecticut state statutes. It is strongly recommended that anybody considering bidding at the tax sale purchase the packet and become familiar with the process before making the decision to bid. City staff will not assist potential bidders by explaining how tax sales work, will not assist in researching properties, and will not offer information about the characteristics of tax sale properties beyond what is available in the packet.
There are bidding restrictions for certain City of Norwalk Employees and Officials who are not permitted to bid at Norwalk tax sales. This policy has been in effect since 2016.

Instructions for Bidders: 9/18/2025 Tax Sale
The City of Norwalk Tax Collector's Office has been charged with collecting unpaid Zoning Violation Fines and unpaid Blight Fees through the use of the Tax Sale process, as authorized by Connecticut General Statute 12-157.
The City has seized these properties and will sell them at public auction on September 18, 2025 , unless the fees and associated collection costs are PAID IN FULL prior to the date of the sale.
In the past, unpaid blight fees have been collected in the same manner as delinquent property taxes. State law has recently been changed (October 1, 2024) to allow the same enforcement for unpaid zoning violation fees.
The City of Norwalk is the first municipality in Connecticut to make use of this enforcement mechanism. The City's goal is to enforce collection of these fees, not necessarily to sell property or to transfer ownership of property from one owner to another. The owners' remedy to avoid the sale of their properties is to pay the fees that are due.
