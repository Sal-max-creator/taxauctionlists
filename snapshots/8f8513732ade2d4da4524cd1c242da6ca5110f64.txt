Tax Collector
The Township of Toms River announces that an online auction of delinquent taxes and other municipal charges for the 2025 tax year and prior years will be held on December 15, 2026, beginning at 9:00 a.m.
For a complete listing of properties, outstanding delinquencies, associated costs, and bidding instructions, please visit
https://liveauctions.govease.com/PublicPortal/RegistrationDetail?AuctionID=1565&Edit=False
All lien redemption request must be faxed to 732-505-1886, please give a redemption date no more than 30 days from the time of request.
Please use the tax account inquiry link
https://wipp.edmundsassoc.com/Wipp/?wippid=1508 to call up the status of a property, check for taxes, open liens and labor charges. If a lien does not show open and certificate of redemption is required to have it cleared from the county clerk&apos;s office, you must submit a request in writing with a check in the amount of $25 for each certificate needed and mail the request to the tax office.
To view your account and print a copy of your tax bill online: Click Here.
You can reach the Tax Collector's office by calling (732) 341-1000 extension 8342. Please keep in mind that we tend to have much higher call volumes during tax time. Please leave a message and we will return your call as soon as possible.
Thank you for your patience.

The Tax Collector is responsible for the billing, collection, reporting, and enforcement of municipal taxes for the Township of Toms River. The Township of Toms River started operating as a calendar year town in January 2010.
Contact the Office
You can reach the Tax Collector's Office by calling 732-341-1000, ext. 8342. Please keep in mind that we tend to have much higher call volumes during tax time. Please leave a message and we will return your call as soon as possible. Thank you for your patience.
Tax Payment Due Dates:
Feb 1st
May 1st
Aug 1st
Nov 1st
There is a 10 day grace period for each due date.
Office Hours: Monday - Friday 8:30am to 4:30pm
After Hours Drop Box - located at the entrance of Town Hall across from the parking garage. The drop box is for tax payments only and will be posted using the next business day.
All redemption requests must be faxed to 732-505-1886, giving a redemption date of no more than 30 days from the time of request.
Please do not email redemption request.
