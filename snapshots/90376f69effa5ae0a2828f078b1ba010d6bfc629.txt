Delinquent Real Estate Taxes Eligible for Land Sale
Many people ask if Spotsylvania County has a list of real estate properties that have delinquent taxes and are eligible for land sale. If taxes on a property are delinquent on December 31 following the second anniversary of the date on which such taxes have become due, these properties may be eligible for land sale. Spotsylvania County has an agreement with the firm of Sands Anderson PC to handle the properties that are eligible for land sale. If you would like more information on the properties eligible for land sale, please contact the office of Margaret F. Hardy located at:

Suite 217
Fredericksburg, VA 22401
