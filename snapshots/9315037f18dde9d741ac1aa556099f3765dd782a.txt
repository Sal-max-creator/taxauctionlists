If your property is involved in a Tax Deed Auction, call 850-438-6500, ext 3324 for information on redeeming your property from the sale.
**IMPORTANT**
Tax Deed Applications filed prior to April 1, 2025, will be redeemed through the Escambia County Clerk of Court. If your property is scheduled for auction BEFORE August 6, 2025, call 850-595-3793 to redeem. For auctions scheduled on or after August 6, 2025, call the Tax Collector at 850-438-6500, ext 3324.
