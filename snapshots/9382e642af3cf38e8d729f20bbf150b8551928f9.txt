Sheriff's Sale
The Sheriff's Sale is conducted on the first Tuesday of each month online through GovEase, at 10 am venue to the highest bidder. All property going to the sheriff's sale is published in the classified section of the Denton Record Chronicle. All information related to the rules and payments for real estate purchased are listed on the GovEase website: www.govease.com .
