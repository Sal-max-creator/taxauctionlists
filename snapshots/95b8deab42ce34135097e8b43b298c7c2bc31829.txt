Porter County 2026 Tax Sale
Advertisement for 2026 Porter County Tax Sale
Porter County&apos;s 2026 Tax Sale will be held online October 21-22, 2026. Additional information will be available in the future.
Per Porter County Ordinance No. 00-7, requests for the results of the information related to the tax sale results can only be made in-person at the Auditor&apos;s office and CANNOT be distributed electronically.
Results of the 2025 Tax Sale are open to examination in-person at the Auditor&apos;s office upon request.
Bidders are strongly encouraged to review their noticing and other requirements. These requirements are outlined in a "Lien Buyer Handout" prepared by the vendor who conducts the tax and certificate sales, SRI Incorporated of Indianapolis.
To view other information about the Porter County tax sale and tax sales in general, go to the SRI webpage .
You can register for the 2026 Tax Sale by clicking here . Registration closes on October 20th with no exceptions for late registrants.
