Delinquent Tax Sale Information
Only the accrued taxes, fees, and penalties are sold at the tax sale. Purchasing taxes is a complex legal procedure. Please be aware that when you purchase at the tax sale, you are purchasing taxes, not property . It is advised that you contact an attorney, and/or review the Illinois Compiled Statutes prior to participating in a tax sale. All dates listed below are subject to change.
1. When will your next tax sale be held?
The delinquent tax sale will be held on Monday, October 27, 2025
2. Where is it held and what time does it begin?
It is held at the DeKalb County Administration Building-Conference Room East, 110 E. Sycamore Street, Sycamore, IL 60178 at 9:00 a.m.
3. When and where do you advertise for the sale?
We advertise in the local newspapers after all second installment payments are posted.
4. Can you send me a list of the liens that will be available at the tax sale?
Lists are available for a $75.00 prepaid fee. You may send your payment at any time. We will then send you a list when they become available, generally 3-4 weeks after the second installment due date. Please see FAQ #6 for information on acceptable payment methods.
5. Do I need to register for the sale and is there a fee for this?
You are required to have your current year Tax Buyer Registration form on file in our office, 10 business days prior to the tax sale. For this year’s sale, all Tax Buyer Registration forms must be turned in to the Treasurer’s Office by October 9, 2026. Please use our DeKalb County Tax Buyer Registration form, which you can request by contacting the Collector’s Office via email treasurer@dekalbcounty.org or by phone 815-895-7112 approximately 2 weeks after the 2nd installment due date. Your registration must be accompanied by a $500.00 deposit, which will be applied to your purchases at the sale. It may also be refunded if you attend the sale, but do not purchase any taxes. Your deposit will be forfeited if you do not attend the sale. One buyer per registration will be allowed to bid at the tax sale.
When registering for the DeKalb County Tax Sale, you are registering with the following understandings:
For the 2025 tax year, the DeKalb County Board has submitted a list of real estate properties to the collector that the county wishes to purchase. This list contains all delinquent real estate parcels.
Tax lien certificates are part of a complex legal process, and purchasing such certificates comes with a variety of risks.
Purchasing tax lien certificates is a specialized form of investment and has specific statutes/codes that govern it. As a tax buyer, you are responsible for knowing the risks and seeking appropriate legal or financial advice for any questions regarding those risks and any/all requirements outlined in governing statutes/codes.
The Tax Lien Certificate process is governed by Illinois State Statutes/Illinois Property Tax Codes, and other regulations. These statutes/codes outline the specific actions that are required for holders of tax lien certificates, and applicable codes, laws, and regulations may periodically change.
It is the tax buyer’s responsibility to exercise due diligence and perform adequate research on the tax lien certificate process, and any real estate parcels or mobile homes they may purchase certificates for. The tax sale is final; any changes must happen during the sale.
Staff from the DeKalb County Treasurer’s Office, Clerk, Assessment Office, and other County departments or offices are not attorneys, and cannot provide legal advice. They are not responsible for or obligated to advise anyone of rights or responsibilities regarding the tax certificate process beyond the scope of their job duties.
6. What form of payment do you accept?
Certified funds, (cash, cashier&#8217;s check, money order, or letter of credit with a check) are required for first-time buyers for both the tax sale list and the deposit. If you are not a first-time buyer, personal, business, or cashier’s checks are accepted. Taxes must be paid immediately after the close of the tax sale.
The Deposit and the list fee may be paid at the same time, or separately. They may also be paid on the same check or separately.
7. What happens to the liens that are not sold at the auction?
All of the liens are sold at the tax sale. For the 2025 tax year, the DeKalb County Board has submitted a list of real estate properties to the collector that the county wishes to purchase. This list contains all delinquent real estate parcels.
8. Will the tax sale be final?
Yes.
9. Do you have any sales scheduled after the next one coming up?
The tax sale only occurs once per year.
10. How is this sale conducted? Can I bid without attending (via mail, or a representative)?
The sale will be conducted in an open outcry format. You or your registered representative must be physically present at the sale to bid. You cannot bid via any other manner.
11. Can you provide me with a list of property use codes?
The Chief County Assessments Office can assist you with property use codes.
12. If I end up with a property, does the county handle the foreclosure process?
It is the tax buyer’s responsibility to follow the procedures necessary to acquire a deed.
13. Will all other liens be cleared from the property as a result of the sale?
No, the tax sale does not clear any liens from the property.
14. Is a copy of the state statutes regarding the tax sale available for purchase?
The tax sale is governed by the Illinois Compiled Statutes which are available at your local library, or online.
15. Where do I call for redemption information?
For redemption information, please contact the DeKalb County Clerk’s office at 815-895-7149 .
