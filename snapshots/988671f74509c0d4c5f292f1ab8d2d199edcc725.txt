Auction Information
We usually hold one public, in-person oral auction per year at the County Courthouse.
To bid, you must appear in person or send a representative.
Properties are offered with a minimum bid listed on the official Auction Listing.
Payment in full is required for items under $10,000; items over $10,000 require a 10% down payment due within five business days.
We issue a Certificate of Sale at the time of purchase and prepare a quitclaim deed within 180 days.
We offer no warranties and no title insurance.
In Oregon, redemption rights end when the property is deeded to Jackson County.
Sign up for e-notify to receive updates and notifications of upcoming auctions.
Select "Real Property Auctions&mdash;Finance Department" when subscribing.
