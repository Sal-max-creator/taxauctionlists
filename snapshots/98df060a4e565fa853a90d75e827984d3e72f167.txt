Judiciary Sale
The Juniata County Tax Claim Bureau conducts Judicial Sales for tax delinquent properties that have been previously exposed but not sold at Upset Sale. The Bureau has the right to set all opening bids and sets Judicial opening bids to cover the cost of collections. All Tax Sales are conducted in accordance with the PA Real Estate Tax Sale Law and are final.
Properties are advertised for sale in one newspaper of general circulation within Juniata County and the Juniata County Law Reporter at least thirty days prior to the sale.
All Sale Properties Are Sold Without Guarantee or Warranty Whatsoever. Please Note That Property Listings Shown Here May Vary up Until the Day of the Sale.
All Tax Sales Are Conducted in Accordance with the Real Estate Tax Sale Law and Directed by the Juniata County Tax Claim Bureau.
