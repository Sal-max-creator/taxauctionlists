Forfeiture Property for Sale
NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that Clay County shall sell to the highest bidder the following described parcels of land that forfeited to the State of Minnesota for non-payment of taxes. These parcels have been classified and appraised as provided by law. The online sale will commence on November 9th, 2026 at 8:00 AM and will close on December 9th, 2026 at 4:30 PM.
Properties are sold to the highest bidder "as is". Sales shall be cash only on individual parcels. In addition to the sales price there shall also be added an amount equal to 3% of such total sales prices, said 3% to be credited to the Tax Forfeited Assurance Account as required under Minnesota Statute 284.28 Sub. 6. A $25.00 state deed fee and a $46.00 recording fee must also be collected for each parcel sold. Each parcel is subject to a state deed tax of $1.65 for sales up to $3,000 or 0.0033 times the total of the purchase price. There will also be an additional $54.00 well certificate fee for any property that contains a well. Purchasers of property at this sale will receive a State Deed.
CLAY COUNTY DOES NOT GUARANTEE CLEAR TITLE ON PURCHASES OF TAX FORFEITED LAND. IT IS THE RESPONSIBILITY OF THE PURCHASER TO TAKE WHATEVER ACTION NECESSARY TO ASSURE CLEAR TITLE. A CERTIFICATE OF OCCUPANCY FROM THE CITY OF MOORHEAD WILL BE REQUIRED TO ANYONE OCCUPYING THE PROPERTY.
Registration is required on the Public Surplus auction site . It is recommended that you research any parcel you wish to purchase before buying and consider contacting your attorney for specific legal questions. Any parcels not sold will be offered again during a 2nd online auction starting on December 14, 2026 at 8AM and will close on December 27, 2026 at 4:30PM.

Sale Information
The Clay County Auditor&apos;s Office holds an online auction sale to dispose of Clay County properties forfeited to the state for nonpayment of taxes. These properties forfeit after 3 years of nonpayment of taxes.
Properties are sold to the highest bidder "as is." The buyer should understand that the properties might not conform to local building and zoning ordinances, and that environmental and/or watershed / water conservation issues may affect the property.
Requirements & Recommendations
Registration is required on the day of the auction. It is recommended that you research any parcel you wish to purchase before buying and consider contacting your attorney for specific legal questions. Parcels unsold will be available over the counter at the Auditor&apos;s Office.

Special Assessments
The special assessments on each property are subject to recertification. These assessments may be recertified by the city or other taxing jurisdiction upon the sale of the property and will be the responsibility of the new owner. Improvements not yet assessed are the responsibility of the purchaser.
Payment Requirements
Payment must be made by Cashier's Check, Certified Check, Personal Check, or Money Order made payable to Clay County Auditor. Previous Default: Payment must be made by certified funds if the purchaser has ever made payment that has been returned non-sufficient funds (NSF).
Amount Due Upon Sale
Winning bidders will be emailed a Buyer/Deed Registration Form upon the close of the auction. This form must be completed and returned to the Auction Contact within 5 days from the close of the auction. This form will also note additional fees, etc., due and will have the total amount due for the property.
Buyer Premium
A Buyer Premium of 5% will be added to the final sale price with a $1 minimum charge per auction to collect payment. The premium will be visible during the bidding process and will be included in the payment required.
Additional Fees

Title
The buyer will complete a Buyer/Deed Registration form at the time of purchase. Once the financial obligations, paperwork, and property conditions are satisfied, a state deed is issued by the MN Department of Revenue. This conveyance shall have the force and effect of a patent from the state. However, services of an attorney may be necessary to make the title marketable.
The State of Minnesota reserves all mineral rights.
