Any parcel with delinquent property tax will be auctioned off through GovEase by online tax lien auction.
Great News!
Houston County Revenue Commission has partnered with GovEase for our annual online tax lien auction that will be held beginning Monday, May 4th, 2026.
For any questions or concerns, please visit the GovEase website at GovEase or call GovEase at 769-208-5050 Option 2 or email their support at support@govease.com
