ONLINE TAX LIEN SALE
Talladega County Tax Lien Sale for 2025 delinquent tax properties will begin on May 12, 2026.
from 9:00 AM - 4:30 PM. and continue from day to day until completed on the GOVEASE website, click here to go to the website.
REGISTRATION & ONLINE WEBINAR TRAINING
Registration is required for all individuals who will participate in the lien sale. There is no charge for registration. Registration begin April 27, 2026.
Please click here to view more information on registration and training.
*Note: To view the instructions, you will need a pdf reader. If you do not already have Adobe Acrobat Reader, you may click here to go to Adobe's web site for a free download.
Disclaimer : The Talladega County Revenue Office will never call and ask a taxpayer for a payment or banking information over the phone. Our staff may call and remind that late property tax payments are due, but no financial information will be asked. Any such information being sought is a scam.
Important Dates Leading Up to the Tax Lien Sale
The last day to pay with a check is March 27, 2026. After this day the only payment accepted is cash, cashiers check, money order or card.
On April 13, 2026, the Talladega Office will start contacting last years bidders to come in and pay any unpaid taxes bought at the lien sale last year. First Right Bidders can pay in Talladega starting April 27, 2026.
The last day to pay property tax is May 8, 2026. Payments can be taken at all locations through May 8, 2026. Anything not paid by this date (May 8, 2026) is considered tax lien sale property and must be redeemed. The last day to redeem prior sales is also May 8, 2026.
Online payments will also be shut down on May 8, 2026.
The tax lien sale is on May 12, 2026.
2025 tax lien sales may be redeemed starting May 25, 2026.
If there are any questions, please contact T alladega County Revenue Office .
