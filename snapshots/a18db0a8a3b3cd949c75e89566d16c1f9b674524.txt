Sheriff Sales
The Wood County Sheriff’s Office conducts the sale of all real estate subject to foreclosure on the “Wood County Ohio Official Foreclosure Auction Site” which is operated by Realauction based on a contract with the Ohio Department of Administrative Services.
The process and procedures under which these sales will be completed are detailed at the Realauction website https://wood.sheriffsaleauction.ohio.gov.
For more information visit the Sheriff's website .
