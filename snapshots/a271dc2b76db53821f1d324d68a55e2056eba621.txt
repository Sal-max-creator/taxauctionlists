MOHAVE COUNTY 2026 TAX LIEN SALE
Click Here for Tax Lien Sale training videos
Attention Lien holder's: In accordance with Federal and State public record laws, any Lien holder's name and address may be released upon request.
OVERVIEW
Tax Lien Certificates will be sold against parcels with 2024 delinquent taxes, and any prior year taxes.
All bidders, including those having current bidder numbers, must register.
ALL PURCHASES MADE AT THE AUCTION ARE FINAL, NO EXCEPTIONS .
Fees :
$10.00 non-refundable processing fee is charged to each certificate (ARS 42-18116C).
$5.00 non-refundable winning bidder fee per parcel is charged by Real Auction.
PARTICIPANTS ARE URGED to research properties prior to purchasing liens, IT IS YOUR
RESPONSIBILITY . It is also the bidder's responsibility to know the laws governing tax lien sales.
If you have questions, you may contact our office:
Phone 928-753-0737
liens@mohave.gov
