The 2026 Campbell County Delinquent Sale will be held Wednesday, August 12, 2026. The last day to pay delinquent taxes is by 4:00 pm on Tuesday, August 11, 2026.

Delinquent Tax Sale info
2025 BILLS SOLD AT 2026 TAX SALE
IF YOU HAVE DELINQUENT TAXES PLEASE NOTE:
that after April 15 in the year after the tax bill is due, a CERTIFICATE OF DELINQUENCY will be created constituting a LIEN OF RECORD against the listed property. Payment of this bill is a personal obligation of the taxpayer. The amount due will increase each month with the accrual of additional interest. Thus, payment is very important.
PROPERTY TAX BILL CERTIFICATES OF DELINQUENCY WILL BE CREATED ON OR ABOUT APRIL 16th EACH YEAR.
The sale of delinquent tax bills by the Campbell County Clerk’s Office WILL BEGIN IN AUGUST OF MOST YEARS. Any Certificate of Delinquency which is not paid in full by that date may be sold to a third party purchaser.
Certificates of Delinquency from previous years may have already been purchased. Certificates of Delinquency that are eligible to be purchased may be subject to being purchased by a third-party at any time. If purchased the third party purchaser may impose substantial additional administrative costs and fees.
Collection action by the Campbell County Attorney’s Office or a third-party may include foreclosure of the property.
The County Attorney can be reached at: 859 491-7700
TO SEE IF YOUR CERTIFICATE OF DELINQUENCY HAS BEEN PURCHASED PLEASE VISIT THE CAMPBELL COUNTY CLERK’S DELINQUENT TAX DEPARTMENT OR CALL 859 292-3845 . IF THE CERTIFICATE OF DELINQUENCY HAS BEEN PURCHASED, THE CAMPBELL COUNTY CLERK’S DELINQUENT TAX DEPARTMENT CAN ALSO GIVE YOU INFORMATION REGARDING THE THIRD PARTY PURCHASER.
For any certificate of delinquency that has not been purchased, payment of the full amount due plus any accrued interest may be made to the County Clerk’s Office.
Payment should be made to:
Campbell County Clerk&#8217;s Office
1098 Monmouth Street,
Newport, KY 41071
The only acceptable form of payment by mail is check or money order. Payment may also be made in person at the County Clerk’s office in which acceptable forms of payment are check or money order.
PAYMENT PLAN INFORMATION
If you are unable to pay a delinquent bill in full, you may qualify for a payment plan with the Campbell County Attorney’s Office. TAX BILLS THAT WERE SOLD AT THE TAX SALE ARE NO LONGER ELIGIBLE FOR PAYMENT PLANS.
For information on available payment plans please contact the Campbell County Attorney’s Office at 859 491-7700 .
