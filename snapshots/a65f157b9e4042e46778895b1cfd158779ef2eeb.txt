Delinquent Real Estate Taxes
Payment Information
As of April 1st of every year - No personal checks will be accepted as payment on any property that is 2 or more year’s delinquent in real estate taxes. Only cash, money orders and certified checks will be accepted.
If you are only 1 year delinquent in real estate taxes, you may use a personal check for payment until April 1st of the following year.
The office will continue to accept one-time installment payment plans for qualified delinquent taxpayers who have not defaulted on prior agreements.
Additional information concerning delinquent taxes can be obtained by calling the Bureau at (814) 317-2361 or by accessing the Blair County web site. Questions concerning your current taxes should be directed to your local tax collector whose contact information can be accessed from the Assessment Office page.
