Tax Sale
The City of Elizabeth's holds an annual Tax Sale for prior fiscal year delinquent municipal charges . The Tax Sale is typically in June; however, contact the office or visit the City of Elizabeth Tax Sale Website for confirmation of the final date.
The Tax Sale is held via an Online Auction. Instructions on bidding, to view items listed for sale and to bid can be found at the City of Elizabeth Tax Sale Website .
