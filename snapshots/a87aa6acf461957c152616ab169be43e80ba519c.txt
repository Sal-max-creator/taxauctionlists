Notice of Upcoming Public Sale of Tax-Forfeited Lands
News & Updates
Posted on October 21, 2026
You are hereby notified that a public online sale of tax-forfeited lands located in Lake County has been scheduled. Parcels on this sale only include those that were forfeited in 2026. No person or entity that is an owner or taxpayer of real property in Lake County that has delinquent property taxes in Lake County may bid on or purchase property at auction or over the counter.
DATE & TIME OF SALE:
EMV SALE:
STARTS: Friday, October 16, 2026 @10:00 AM
CLOSES: Monday, November 16, 2026 @ 10:00 AM
MINIMUM BID SALE:
STARTS: Tuesday, November 17, 2026 @ 10:00 AM
CLOSES: Monday, November 30, 2026 @ 10:00 A M
View and bid at:
https://www.publicsurplus.com/sms/lakecore,mn/list/current?orgid=919229
A copy of the list of land to be sold and the terms of the sale may be obtained from the Lake County Land & Forestry on the Lake County website at:
https://www.lakecountymn.gov/217/Land-Sales
