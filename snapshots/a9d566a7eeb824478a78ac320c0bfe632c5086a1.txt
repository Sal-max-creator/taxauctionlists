Tax Collector
The Tax Collector is charged with the responsibility for receiving and collecting all property taxes and assessments, both current and delinquent. As a statutory officer of the State of New Jersey, the Tax Collector is obligated to follow all State Statutes regarding property tax collection, including billing, due dates, interest on delinquent tax payments, and tax sale procedures. Alison Varrelmann is licensed by the State of New Jersey as a Certified Tax Collector.
It is important to note that the municipal taxes are only a portion of a property taxpayer&apos;s obligation. The two other separate government entities, Ocean County and the Lacey Township Board of Education, develop and adopt their own budgets funded by property taxes. After collection, payments are made to Ocean County and the Lacey Township Board of Education for their portion of taxes. The balance of the collections is used to fund local municipal expenses. New Jersey Law requires that Municipal Government serves as a billing and collection agency for all separate entities.
Your tax bill is mailed out once annually by the end of the summer after the certified rate is approved by the County Board of Taxation. Property taxes are due on February 1, May 1, August 1, and November 1. Failure to receive a tax bill does not exempt you from paying taxes or the interest due on delinquent taxes. The Township does permit a 10-day grace period. The State of New Jersey does not permit postmarks; taxes must be received in the office on or before the 10th. However, if the 10th falls on a weekend or legal holiday, you have until the next business day to make payment without interest. All payments may be made by cash, check, or money order. Credit card payment will be accepted until November 10th for current year taxes.
Bills may be paid in full by adding the amounts on the four numbered stubs. This covers the last half of the current year and the first half of the following year. Visa, MasterCard, Amex and Discover Cards are accepted.
Note : Credit cards can be used for payment of taxes until the property is subject to a tax sale.
We have installed a drop-box (black box), located in front of the building next to the U.S. Post Office mailbox for Township payments or correspondence for your convenience after hours. For payments to be credited the same day, they must be in the drop box no later than 4 pm of that business day.
Any open tax, water, or sewer balance as of November 11th of the current year is subject to tax sale. The tax sale list will be available via a link on this website. A notice will be advertised in the Times-Beacon (a Gannett publication), a weekly paper available on Thursdays.
FAQs
When are tax bills mailed?
Tax bills are mailed out once a year late June or early July. New bills are mailed when there is a change in ownership.
When are tax payments due?
Property taxes are billed in Lacey on a calendar year, from January 1st to December 31st. The first Quarter covers January through March, the second April through June, the third July through September, and the forth October through December. Payments are due in the middle of each quarter or February 1st, May 1st, August 1st, and November 1st (there is a 10-day grace period, on the 11th day, interest is calculated back to the 1st. If the 10th falls on a weekend or legal holiday, you have until the next working day to make payment. We cannot accept postmarks. Failure to receive a tax bill does not exempt you from paying taxes or the interest due on delinquent taxes.
How can I pay my taxes?
Tax payments may be made with a check, cash, money order, or credit card (Visa, MasterCard, Discover Card, and American Express). A convenience fee of 2.95% will be charged by your credit card company. Payment may be made online by E-Check for a flat fee of $1.95. Credit card payments can be made at the tax office or online at this website. Please refer to Online Services and Information then click View and Pay Property Tax on our home page.
Do not mail credit card information to us.
We cannot accept credit card payments after November 10th for current year taxes. Payments after November 28th must be made by cash/certified check or money order only . Any open balances after November 11th of the current year are subject to our tax sale.
Property owners may now sign up for Automated Clearing House (ACH) payments:
Property owners may fill out a short form and provide a voided check at the tax office, which will allow the Township to make a direct withdrawal (at no additional cost to the property owner or the township) from the taxpayer&apos;s checking account. ACH payment may be rescinded by the property owner in writing at any time prior to the quarterly tax due date.
