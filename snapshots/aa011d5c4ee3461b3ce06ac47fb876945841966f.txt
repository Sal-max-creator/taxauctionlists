Tax Sale
This year&apos;s delinquent tax sale date has been set for August 27, 2026 at 9:00 am (CDT). The last day to register and submit lists and fees with the Henderson County Clerk is August 17, 2026. The bill lists must be submitted to sfloyd@hendersoncountyky.gov on an Excel spreadsheet in .xls format. The list must be in the order of preference in which the bills are to be purchased. It is preferable that you use our registration form as it has more information pertinent to our specific tax sale. The Excel spreadsheet must provide the following information in this particular order:
a. County Map or Parcel number
b. Tax bill number
c. Tax Year
d. Taxpayer name
*See section #5 on our registration form for additional information needed on Priority bills.
The tax sale registration form for this year can be found on this page under the delinquent tax bill lists.
Call our office at 270-826-3906 and ask for Sharon Floyd or Gabby Moore to request a registration form and we will be happy to help answer any questions.
