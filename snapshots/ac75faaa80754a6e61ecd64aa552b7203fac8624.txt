Tax Sale
As required by Mississippi Code Section 27-41-49 to 27-41-89, the Tax Collectors of the 82 counties and those municipalities that collect their own taxes must "sell" all land on which the taxes are not paid on either the first Monday in April or the last Monday in August. DeSoto County, like most counties, holds their Tax Sale on the last Monday in August each year .
For more information about DeSoto County&apos;s tax sale, please contact the Tax Collector&apos;s office at 662-469-8030.
