Forefeited Land Sale
Overview
Properties that are tax-foreclosed and remain unsold after two separate Sheriff's Sales are forfeited to the State of Ohio. The Auditor's Office conducts an annual sale of these properties, typically held in August.
2026 Sale Information
The forfeited land sale that had been planned for 2026 was canceled because there were no parcels available for sale.
Registration Information
To pre-register, please email realestate@bcohio.gov and request a registration form.
If you choose not to pre-register, we recommend arriving at least 30 minutes before the sale begins to register onsite.
