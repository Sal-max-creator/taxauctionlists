Sheriff Sale Foreclosures
TRUMBULL COUNTY SHERIFF SALES
There have been changes made to the Sheriff Sales Policies and Procedures.
Please read in full before bidding at an In-Person or Online Sale.
Have Questions? Call 330-675-2854 or email
sheriffsale@co.trumbull.oh.us
BANK FORECLOSURES INFORMATION
The Trumbull County Sheriff’s Office will no longer be conducting in-person
mortgage foreclosures under House Bill 390. Real Auction has been contracted as
the official public sheriff sale website. The process and procedures under which
these sales will be completed are detailed on
Real Auction’s website
.
All prospective bidders should familiarize themselves with this process.
Training is also available for bidders on their site.
These changes do not affect tax sales. Tax sales will still be held in person.
TAX FORECLOSURES INFORMATION
These rules will be in effect as of May 7, 2026.
Sheriff Sales for Tax Foreclosures will be held in person every other Thursday
at 1:30 PM in the Commissioner’s Hearing Room, 160 High St. NW, Warren,
OH 44481. All properties and descriptions will be advertised in the Trumbull
County Legal News 3 weeks prior to the sale.
We recommend you do not solely rely upon any information on the Auditor’s
website. Please drive by any property that you are interested in so you know
the condition of it before bidding. All sales are final. Being on the property
without the permission of the homeowner or occupant is a violation of the
Ohio Revised Code.
Check with the appropriate city, township or village to make sure the property
you are interested in is not on the demolition list. You should also check
with the utility departments to make sure there are no additional liens.
Bidding will begin at the minimum bid amount and will increase in $100 increments.
If you are the successful bidder, a deposit of $3,000.00 will be required
at the time the bidding concludes. The deposit must be in the form of
certified funds made payable to the Trumbull County Sheriff's Office.
In the event that the deposit is not tendered at the time the bid is accepted,
the selling officer shall re-open the bidding immediately, starting with the
minimum bid, and continuing until a sale is made to a bidder who satisfies
the deposit requirement.
Along with the deposit, the purchaser must fill out a Purchaser Information Form.
The form must be legible and completed, as the form will become part of the
court record. Blank forms are available to download here:
PURCHASER INFORMATION FORM
.
30 days from the sale date, a Confirmation of Sale will be filed with the Clerk
of Court. The debtor has the right of redemption of the property until the
confirmation is signed by the Judge.
The balance on the purchase price is due 30 days from the Confirmation of Sale.
Once the balance is received, the Sheriff’s Office will record the deed
transferring the property into the successful bidder’s name.
Please be aware that the Sheriff’s Office does not have access to any homes
or have keys to any properties offered for sale. If the successful bid is not
enough to cover all costs, the buyer is responsible.
