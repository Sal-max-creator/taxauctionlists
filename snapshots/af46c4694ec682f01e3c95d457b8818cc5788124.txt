Standard Online Tax Sale
2026
In New Jersey, all municipalities are required to hold at least one tax sale per year. The properties on which liens are sold at the tax sale are those with delinquent property taxes and/or municipal charges. (Pursuant to N.J.S.A 54:5-19). The Borough of Essex Fells will hold its annual online standard tax lien sale on Wednesday, November 18, 2026 at 10:00 a.m.
All delinquencies for the 2025 tax year are now subject to the tax-sale process.
Please visit https://essexfells.newjerseytaxsale.com for a listing and registration.
Per this process, delinquencies will be subject to a 2% cost of tax sale (up to $100). In addition, delinquencies will be subject to a mailing fee of $25 for each mailing. Two mailings are scheduled during this process.
After October 16, 2026, the amount owed is calculated as follows:
Delinquent Taxes/Other Municipal Charges + Interest + Cost of Sale + Mailing Fees = Total Amount Owed
To receive the exact amount owed, please contact the Tax Office at 973-226-3400 or email us at assistant@essexfellsboro.com .
All payments received after October 16, 2026, MUST be made using guaranteed funds:
cash,
cashier’s check or
money order.
Any other forms of payment WILL NOT be accepted. All other checks – attorney, business, lender, and personal – WILL be returned . In addition, the credit/debit card payment options WILL NOT be available after October 16, 2026.
The statute also requires a Public Notice of Tax Sale. If payment is not received by October 16, 2026 the property owner’s name will appear in this Public Notice.
Note: Failure of the property owner to receive a notice of tax sale properly mailed by the Tax Collector shall not constitute grounds to void the subsequent tax sale.
