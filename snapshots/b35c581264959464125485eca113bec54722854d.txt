Tax Sales
S ales / Auctions of parcels held in trust by the Treasurer for non-payment of taxes are scheduled on an "as needed" basis. When a date for a sale is established by the Treasurer and approved by the Board of County Commissioners, the date of the sale will be published on this site.
A County Trust Property Sale has been scheduled for November 19, 2025 through November 21, 2025 and will be hosted online at www.bid4assets.com/pershing. No in person bidding will take place.
If you wish to be placed on our mailing list and receive notice of the sale, please send an email to clerk-tres@pershingcountynv.gov .
