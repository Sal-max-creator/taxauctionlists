BIDDERS: FOR 2026&apos;S TAX SALES WE HAVE CHANGED ONLINE AUCTION VENDORS TO GOVEASE.COM
General Information
The Upset Sale is held each September by the Bucks County Tax Claim Bureau in accordance with Pennsylvania&apos;s Real Estate Tax Sale Law (RETSL). There is a continued Upset Sale as well as a Judicial Sale in December of each year. All of Tax Claim&apos;s sales are conducted virtually through GovEase, the County&apos;s approved third-party vendor, at https://www.govease.com/
Properties with delinquent real estate taxes that are two years past due may be eligible for the Upset Sale. Eligible properties are advertised in two newspapers of general circulation within the County of Bucks and the Bucks County Law Reporter prior to the sale.
BIDDER REGISTRATION INFORMATION
The list of eligible properties may vary up until the actual day of sale. It is recommended that potential buyers seek the advice of an attorney and research individual properties prior to bidding.
Please note: figures for the Upset Price are approximate, and subject to change due to us receiving more liens.
