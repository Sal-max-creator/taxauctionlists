Voting Location Updates
Voting Location Updates
The drop box at Town Hall has been closed due to flooding.
We will have a mobile drop box at
10 Solomon Drive (Parking lot of Mountain Spirits Liquor and Feather Your Nest) during the following hours
Monday 8:00 a.m. &#8211; 4:00 p.m. and
Election Day, November 3, 2026, 7:00 a.m. &#8211; 7:00 p.m.
Please look for the Archuleta County vehicle that will have a bipartisan team of judges and a sealed box collecting ballots.
The drop box at the TARA Community Center located at 333 Milton Lane in Arboles is still available until Election Day at 7:00 p.m.
The VSPC at the Ross Aragon Community Center will now be located at the Archuleta County Court House located at 109 Harman Park Drive, Pagosa Springs, CO. They will be open 7:00 a.m. &#8211; 7:00 p.m. on Election Day ONLY.
The VSPC at the Archuleta County Extension Building located at 344 Hwy 84 will be open as planned at 7:00 a.m. &#8211; 7:00 p.m. on Election Day ONLY.
The main VSPC at the Archuleta County Election Office located at 305 Village Drive will be open Monday, November 2nd, 2026, 8:00 a.m. &#8211; 4:00 p.m.
and on Election Day, November 3rd, 2026 7:00 a.m. &#8211; 7:00 p.m. You can drop off your mail ballot or vote in person.
We have a Joint La Plata/Archuleta County VSPC located at Pine River Library located at 395 Bayfield Center Drive, Bayfield, CO for Archuleta County residents to vote in person ONLY on Election Day.
Thank you for your understanding and patience as we navigate these unprecedented conditions. Stay safe Archuleta County.

Tax Lien Sale
The 2026 Tax Lien Sale will be conducted on Thursday, November 5, 2026.
To register and access the latest information go to Archuleta County Colorado Official Tax Lien Auction Site .
The tax lien website will go live on October 1, 2026. Investors will then be able to register, view current delinquent accounts, access bidding rules and sign up for the Real Auction webinar training.
The Real Property Tax Lien Sale will be hosted online at Archuleta County Colorado Official Tax Lien Auction Site .
Delinquent account lists will be available below once the newspaper publishes them.
Delinquent Real Property: DELINQUENT TAXES 2026 RP PUBLICATION LIST
Lien purchasers are required to complete a W-9 Form at the time of registration.
Visit County Held Liens for liens that are available for purchase.
