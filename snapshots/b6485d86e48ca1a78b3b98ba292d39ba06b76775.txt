NO PERSONAL OR BUSINESS CHECKS WILL BE ACCEPTED.
Certain Debit/Credit Cards will also be accepted in person with proper ID. Additional fees will be applied.

The Tax Claim Bureau is responsible for the collection
of all delinquent real property taxes on behalf of the
County, Municipalities, and School Districts located
in Dauphin County. The Bureau does not collect
delinquent taxes for the following taxing entities:
Steelton Borough and Steelton-Highspire School District beginning with the 2018 tax and Highspire Borough beginning with the 2024 tax are collected by Portnoff Law Associates.
Lower Paxton Township delinquent real property tax is collected by Portnoff Law Associates beginning with the 2024 tax.
Portnoff Law Associates can be reached at 1-
866-211-9466 with any questions or concerns.
You can also visit their website at
www.portnoffonline.com .
Penbrook Borough delinquent real property tax is collected by CGA Law Firm beginning with the 2024 tax.
CGA Law Firm can be reached at 717-848-4900,
ext. 131 with any questions or concerns.
In this statutory collection process, the Bureau
receives unpaid accounts from the 39 Municipalities,
via tax collectors and/or treasurers within Dauphin
County, provides statutorily mandated notices to
owners, and administers Upset Tax Sales, Judicial Tax
Sales, bankruptcies, and the Repository of unsold
properties. The Bureau also provides various tax
certifications regarding paid and unpaid delinquent
taxes as requested.
Tax Certification
A tax certification is an official document produced on Tax Claim Bureau letterhead stating whether there are or are not delinquent real estate taxes due on a parcel.
A tax claim statement accompanies this document, which reflects the amount of delinquent taxes owed on that parcel as of the certificate date. If no delinquent taxes are due, the claim statement will reflect that information.
The cost for a tax certification is $15 per parcel.
Tax Sale Abstract
A Tax Sale Abstract is an official document produced by the Tax Claim Bureau after the tax sale of a parcel. An abstract includes the date and type of sale, owners name(s), sale notice dates and type of service, posting and advertisement dates and the purchasers name(s).
An Upset Sale Abstract provides information regarding the notification and type of service to owner(s) and co-owner(s).
A Judicial Sale Abstract provides the same information as the Upset Sale Abstract, as well as lien holder(s) and type of service of sale notification.
A Repository Sale Abstract provides the same information as Judicial Sale Abstracts.
The cost for a tax sale abstract is $25 per parcel, per sale.
