Delinquent Tax Sale Information & Provisions
The Teton County Treasurer annual delinquent tax sale is generally held during the first two weeks of August.
For the 2025 tax year, the sale was Wednesday, August 5, 2026. There were 11 parcels and 45 participants. Thanks to all who participated. All parcels were sold at the sale.
2026 Tax Year Sale
For the 2026 tax year, the sale will be Wednesday, August 4, 2027.
For a period of three consecutive weeks, four weeks prior to the sale, properties to be sold are published in the county legal newspaper:
Jackson Hole News and Guide
P.O. Box 7445
Jackson, WY 83001
Public notice and listings are provided through the publication, separate mailing lists are not available.
The sale is held at ***PLEASE NOTE VENUE*** :
Teton County Fairgrounds Community Building
305 W. Snow King
Jackson, WY 83001
**ALSO: Payment by check and credit can be made at the tax sale this year! Cash payments still need to be made at the office.**
Teton County Tax Sale Information
Pre-Registration
You must be pre-registered to attend the tax sale - There will be no registration the morning of tax sale. Pre-registration will take place on Monday, August 2, 2027 and Tuesday, August 5, 2027, at the County Treasurer Office in the County Admin Building, 200 South Willow St, from 9 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
Each pre-registered participant will receive an assigned number. You must have this number to enter the Teton County Fairgrounds Community Building on the morning of the Tax Sale - no exceptions.
The tax sale will begin promptly at 9 a.m., late arrivals will not be entitled to take part.
The name on the pre-registration form must be the same name under which payment will be made. The purchaser at a tax sale receives a certificate of purchase, not title to the real property identified.
Only individuals who are pre-registered and present may participate, neither representatives nor anyone under the age of 18, are allowed. Each pre-registered purchaser will be assigned a number. The numbers shall be randomly drawn using a computer software program. The person will then be permitted to complete the purchase of the tax lien for the parcel according to law.
Any real property which cannot be sold for the amount of taxes, interest, penalties and costs shall be bid in for the County by the County Treasurer.
Payments
Only cash, bank checks or Discover, American Express, Master Card or Visa credit cards accepted; all credit card payments are subject to an additional convenience fee.
No realty companies, company names, or corporate names will be accepted on checks or credit cards.
Owner redemption and other specific statutory provisions relating to the overall tax sale process can be found under Wyoming Statutes &sect;39-13-108(e). Office personnel are not authorized to provide legal advice, nor answer questions pertaining to legality issues.
Thank you for your anticipated participation.
