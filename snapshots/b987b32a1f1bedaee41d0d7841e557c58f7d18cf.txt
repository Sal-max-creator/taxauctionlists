What are the date, time and location of your tax sale? How often is it held?
The tax sale is held annually online via the Real Auction website.
Finance - Property Tax - Tax Sale
How is the tax sale advertised?
The tax sale is advertised in a local paper of general circulation for 4 weeks prior to the sale.
Finance - Property Tax - Tax Sale
When and how do I register for the sale?
There is a $100 nonrefundable bidder registration fee. Registration is open from May 16th until May 23rd at 4 pm.
Cecil County, MD - Official Tax Certificate Auction Site
Finance - Property Tax - Tax Sale
What are the payment requirements? Do you require a deposit?
The amount of taxes, interest, and other associated fees is due the day of the sale by 4 pm via ACH, plus any applicable bid premium. See the question on Bid Premium information. The balance of the bid will be required upon foreclosure.
Finance - Property Tax - Tax Sale
What is the bidding process?
Online bidding begins May 31st at 8 am and closes on June 3rd at noon. Cecil County will use the bid premium method as per section 14-817(b)(2)(i) of the Tax Property Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland.
Finance - Property Tax - Tax Sale
What is &#x201C;Bid Premium&#x201D;?
The bid premium system was designed to curb the excessive and chaotic bidding that has occurred in several jurisdictions throughout the state. If the successful bid exceeds 40% of the full cash value of a property, the bidder will be required to pay a 20% premium on the amount by which the bid exceeds that 40%.
For example, if the full cash value is $150,000 and the high bid is $70,000, the buyer would pay a premium of $2,000, calculated as follows:
40% of $150,000 (full cash value/FCV) equals $60,000.
$70,000 (bid) minus $60,000 (40% FCV) equals $10,000.
20% of $10,000 equals $2,000.
The bid premium will be returned without interest to the buyer if the tax sale certificate is redeemed or if foreclosure is executed within 2 years of the tax sale.
If the property is neither redeemed nor foreclosed, the County retains the bid premium.
Finance - Property Tax - Tax Sale
What type of document is issued after the sale?
Immediately after the sale, you will be issued a cash receipt showing the amount paid. On or before September 30th, you will receive a Certificate of Sale for Unpaid Property Taxes on properties that have an open lien.
Finance - Property Tax - Tax Sale
What is the foreclosure process? Will the County handle the foreclosure process for a fee?
The successful bidder may begin foreclosure proceedings six months from the date of tax sale if the owner has not redeemed the property. Upon foreclosure, the balance of the bid is due to the County. The County will not handle foreclosure proceedings, and it is suggested you contact your attorney for additional information.
Finance - Property Tax - Tax Sale
What happens to liens that are not sold at tax sale? Can they be purchased directly from the County?
If a property is not sold at tax sale, it may be purchased over the counter. However, in recent years, all liens have been sold at tax sale, and we expect this trend to continue. Email our office to receive any information regarding available properties for over-the-counter purchase.
