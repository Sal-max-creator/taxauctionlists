Tax Lien Sales
The Commonwealth of Virginia does not authorize the transfer of title to real estate upon payment of delinquent taxes (tax lien sales). The City of Chesapeake does conduct judicial sales of property through public auctions for nonpayment of taxes in accordance with Code of Virginia Section 58.1-3965 et seq. Information on any upcoming sales, if scheduled, will be posted below.
For more information concerning the rules of sale of property for delinquent taxes and the legal group conducting those sales, visit the Rules of Sale of Property for Delinquent Taxes page.
