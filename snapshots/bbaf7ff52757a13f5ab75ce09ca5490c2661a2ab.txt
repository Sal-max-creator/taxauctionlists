NOTICE TO ALL TAX BUYERS: Pursuant to Public Act 104-0553 , Clinton County has elected to acquire all delinquent real estate property taxes as trustee for the taxing districts beginning with the 2025 PAYABLE IN 2026 tax sale. Delinquent mobile home taxes will continue to be offered at the tax sale to private tax buyers under the applicable provisions of Illinois law.
Clinton County Delinquent Tax Sale &#8211; Mobile Homes will be held on December 28, 2026, 10:00 am in the Clinton County Boardroom, 810 Franklin St, Carlyle IL. Catalogs are available for purchase at the Clinton County Treasurer&#8217;s Office, County Courthouse, 850 Fairfax St, Carlyle IL, 62231 If you have any questions, please contact the Treasurer&#8217;s office.
Note the following:
Bidders must be pre-registered, and only pre-registered bidders will be admitted. The pre-registration deadline is December 14th, 2026.
Bidders may only bid for the company they registered with.

Taxpayer&#8217;s Update
Please Note: Taxpayers can pay their Real Estate tax bills by mail, at local banks, or at the Treasurer&#8217;s office.
If paying by mail or at the Treasurer&#8217;s office, make checks payable to &#8220;Clinton County Treasurer.&#8221;
Address: P.O. Box 174, Carlyle, IL 62231-0174.
If paying by mail; your cancelled check is your receipt.
If you want a receipt, please send a self-addressed stamped envelope along with your payment.
If paying by mail, send check and appropriate stub #1 or #2, in order to receive proper credit for your payment.
If paying at a local bank or at the Treasurer&#8217;s office, bring check and appropriate stub #1 or #2 along to receive proper credit for your payment.
Please examine bills carefully before paying them.
If this bill is no longer your responsibility, please send it back to our off ice.
If you own your home, and are presently living at this residence, be sure that you are receiving your &#8220;GENERAL HOMESTEAD EXEMPTION.&#8221;
If you have improved your home you may qualify for an &#8220;IMPROVEMENT EXEMPTION&#8221;.
If your payment is late; Illinois law requires a 1 1/2% per month interest penalty on each installment.
To avoid late penalty charges, your tax bill must be paid before each due date.
For TIF information regarding previous, current or future projects please visit the Illinois State Comptroller&#8217;s website at SearchForm &#8211; The Illinois Office of Comptroller
Early (Pre-Pay) Tax Payments: If you would like to pre-pay your taxes please send payment with your parcel number(s) and we will credit your taxes when the bills are sent.
Tax bills will be mailed out August 14, 2026.
Due Dates: September 16th, 2026 & October 28th, 2026
If you want to register to receive your tax bill electronically go to https://clinton.mcgedoc.com and enter your registration code which is printed on your tax statement.
