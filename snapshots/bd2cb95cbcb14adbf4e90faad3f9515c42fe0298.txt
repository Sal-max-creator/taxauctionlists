About the Tax Lien Sale
Each year, failure to pay property tax results in a Tax Lien Auction of the property. For more information, please call the Revenue Commissioner's Office at (205) 384-7267 .
The Tax Lien Auction is held online at GovEase on the first Monday in May each year, pending any unforeseen or emergency changes. Registration for the auction is open 30 days prior to auction day and must be completed by 4:00 p.m. CST the Friday before the auction on GovEase's website.
Deadlines for Paying Taxes
Taxpayers may pay online beginning October 1, 2026.
You may pay by credit or debit card over the phone after October 10 each year by calling (205) 384-7267 .
Walker County, Alabama taxes are delinquent after December 31. Penalties and interest begin accruing on January 1.
After February 15 , payment of delinquent taxes must be made by cash, certified check, money order, or credit/debit card. Personal or business checks will not be accepted after that date.
A $0.30 or 2.5% service fee (whichever is greater) is applied to all credit and debit card transactions. We are unable to process payments by electronic check.
