2026 Tax Sale Information
The 2026 Tax Sale, for unpaid prior year municipal charges, will be held electronically and will conclude at 8:00 AM on December 2, 2026.
The Tax Sale auction website is: https://eastbrunswick.newjerseytaxsale.com/
Effective 9/14/26, all payments to Stay Out of the Tax Sale must be made with cash, money order or certified funds. Online payments will not be permitted.
On 10/13/26, Cost of Sale charges will be applied to accounts flagged for Tax Sale.
The Tax Sale will be advertised on the Township Website from 10/31/26 through 12/2/26 and will be advertised in The Home News Tribune during the weeks of 11/2/26 and 11/9/26.
The first $25.00 In-Lieu of Advertising charge will be applied to accounts flagged for Tax Sale on 11/16/26.
The second $25.00 In-Lieu of Advertising charge will be applied to accounts flagged for Tax Sale on 11/23/26.
PAYMENTS TO STAY OUT OF TAX SALE MUST BE RECEIVED IN THE EAST BRUNSWICK TAX COLLECTOR'S OFFICE BY 4:00 PM ON 12/1/26.

Pay by Bank ACH Direct Withdrawal
Set it and Forget it! You are able to pay your property tax and/or water & sewer utility bills using ACH Direct Withdrawal from your bank account FREE of charge. Complete the Authorization Agreement for ACH Direct Withdrawals form below or access the form by scanning the QR code below. Completed forms must be received no later than 15 days prior to the payment due date. Once you submit the form payments will be automatically deducted from your bank account on every subsequent due date.

Payments Made by Check
If you pay by check, please make sure that:
The written amount matches the numerical amount.
The check is signed.
The check is not post-dated.
Checks that are not written correctly, are not signed or are post-dated will be returned.
Final Meter Readings
If you are selling your home and require a final water/sewer bill, please email the following to tax@eastbrunswick.org at least 48 hours prior to closing:
A clear picture of your water meter showing all digits; your water meter is located inside your home usually in a basement or utility closet.
The property address.
The date of the closing.
The seller&apos;s name.
The buyer&apos;s name and contact information (phone & email).
When sending your email, copy all parties who you would like to receive a copy of the final utility bill. The final bill will be calculated and sent via "Reply All" email. Please note that it may take up to 48 hours to calculate your final bill.

Tax Payment Due Dates
Tax payments are due quarterly: February 1st, May 1st, August 1st and November 1st. There is always a ten-day grace period. If the 10th falls on a weekend or holiday, the grace period extends to the next business day. Payments received after the end of the grace period will be charged interest back to the statutory due date. Postmarks are not accepted as date of receipt.
