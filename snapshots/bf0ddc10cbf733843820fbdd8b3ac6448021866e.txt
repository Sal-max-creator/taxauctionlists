Tax Collector
Office of the Tax Collector
The Office of the Tax Collector provides the necessary cash to fund City services with timely billing and collection of both tax and sewer bills. They&apos;re also responsible for auctioning delinquent tax, sewer, and miscellaneous charges during the annual Tax Sale.
DROP BOX FOR PAYMENT: A mail/payment lockbox is now located in front of the entrance of City Hall. The Tax Collector will empty lockbox Monday through Friday. Payments dropped off after 3:00 pm will be processed the next business day. NO CASH PLEASE .
Taxes Due
Taxes are due quarterly on:
- February 1st, May 1st, August 1st and November 1st with a ten day grace period
Pay by Mail
Please mail all payments to the City of Clifton&apos;s m ailing address :
City of Clifton
Attention: Tax Department
900 Clifton Avenue
Clifton, NJ 07013
Pay Online
Only e-checks are acceptable modes of payment. Please enter the correct routing number and checking account number when processing your payment. A $35 returned check fee will be imposed if the account numbers are incorrect.
