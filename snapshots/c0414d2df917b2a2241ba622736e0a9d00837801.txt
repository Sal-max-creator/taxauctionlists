Delinquent Tax Sales
Smith County does not sell tax lien certificates. Investors may purchase properties at our tax sales.
Tax Sale Information
Requirement
Completed written statement of no taxes due from the Smith County Tax Assessor-Collector&apos;s office.
Complete the Request for Written Statement (PDF) under Texas Tax Code &sect;34.015 Regarding Delinquent Taxes. List all properties owned by you in Smith County or any city or any school district that is located at least in part in Smith County.
Include your email address on the mailing address line.
Have the completed form notarized. Wait to sign the form until you are before the notary so they may witness your signature.
Submit the notarized form with $10 to the Smith County Tax Office to request a written statement.
Date & Time
First Tuesday of every month at 10 am.
Location
Smith County Official Tax Foreclosure Sales
To Obtain a Tax Sale List
A tax sale list will be published in the Tyler Morning Telegraph on three occasions during the two weeks preceding the sale (dates vary). Notices are also posted at the Smith County Courthouse. These tax attorneys represent Smith County:
Linebarger Goggan Blair & Sampson, LLP
Post-tax sale property listings in various jurisdictions of Smith County.
Perdue, Brandon, Fielder, Collins & Mott
Only represent the Tyler Independent School District.
Payment
Payment in full is required of the winning bidder on the day of the sale. Payment must be by cashier&apos;s check or money order. Credit cards are not accepted.
Other Costs
The other costs that must be paid at the time of sale are:
Any fees that have not been paid
Other costs associated with the sale
Taxes for post-judgment years may also be due from the buyer
Deed
A sheriff&apos;s deed is issued at the time of sale, or shortly thereafter.
Redemption
The owner may redeem the property at any time up to six months to two years after the sale. The redemption period varies for each property according to the type of property, the length of time we have been trustees, etc. When an owner redeems the property, he must pay the investor the amount paid at the time of sale plus 25% and any costs of sale during the first year of redemption. During the second year, the investor is entitled to the amount paid at the time of sale plus 50% and any costs of sale.
