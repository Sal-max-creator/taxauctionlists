Jefferson County Tax Lien Sale
Treasurer's Tax Lien Sale
The tax lien sale is the final step in the treasurer&apos;s efforts to collect taxes on real property. A tax lien is placed on every county property owing taxes on January 1 each year and remains until the property taxes are paid. If the property owner does not pay the property taxes by late October, the county sells the tax lien at the annual tax lien sale. The tax lien is auctioned to the highest bidder, who then becomes the tax lien certificate holder. The investor that holds the lien will be notified every August of any unpaid taxes and can endorse those taxes to their existing lien.
The tax lien sale allows taxing authorities to receive their budgeted revenue without having to wait for delinquent taxes to be collected. It also provides an investment opportunity for the general public, members of which can purchase tax lien certificates that can potentially earn an attractive interest rate.
The property owner has three years to redeem the tax lien before the lawful holder of the certificate of purchase of the tax lien may apply for a public auction for the sale of a certificate of option for treasurer&apos;s deed.
When redeeming a tax lien, the property owner pays the the delinquent taxes as well as the delinquent interest that has accrued against the lien since it was sold at tax sale, this is credited to the tax lien holder. Please contact the Jefferson County Treasurer 303-271-8330 to obtain payoff information. For tax liens we only accept cash or certified funds (e.g. Cashier's Check, money order, or wire) payable to Jefferson County Treasurer. Payments need to be received in our office by 5 p.m. the last business day of the month, postmarks are not accepted for tax liens. Jefferson County offices are closed on Fridays.
Tax Lien Sale FAQs
What happens if I don’t pay my property taxes?
A tax lien is placed on every county property owing taxes on January 1 each year and remains until the property taxes are paid. If the property owner does not pay the property taxes, the county treasurer sells the tax lien at the annual tax lien sale that takes place in November. The tax lien sale is the final step in the treasurer&apos;s efforts to collect taxes on real property. The tax lien is auctioned to the highest bidder, who then becomes the tax lien certificate holder. The treasurer sells the unpaid taxes not the property. Call 303-271-8330 or contact us for more information.
How long do I have before the property tax is advertised for tax sale?
Delinquent real estate taxes are advertised for three consecutive weeks beginning in September. The advertising dates vary from year to year, and it&apos;s best to pay before September 1 to avoid the advertising charge. Call 303-271-8330 or contact us for more information.
Is there an additional fee if my delinquent taxes are advertised?
Yes, we will add an advertising fee to the property taxes. Call 303-271-8330 or contact us for more information.
