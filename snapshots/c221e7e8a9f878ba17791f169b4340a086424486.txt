Buying Borough Land
Find detailed information and explore the processes for how Borough-owned land is made available for sale through auctions, over-the-counter, or direct sales.
About the Borough Land Sales
Quick Facts
A goal of our Division is to transfer unretained public lands into private ownership through sales.
Residents may apply to nominate Borough lands for future sale. See Nominations page for details.
Any sale for Borough-owned land must first be approved by the Borough Assembly through ordinance.
Prices are based on market appraisal and must be approved by the Borough Assembly prior to listing.
Types of Land Sales
Select the tabs below to learn more about the types of land uses permitted.
Sealed-Bid Auction
After the Borough Assembly authorizes a nominated land sale, the property can be made available through a sealed-bid auction.
How Does a Sealed Bid Auction Work?
Minimum bid amounts are based on fair market value as determined by independent appraisal.
Highest Bid wins! There are no cash discounts.
All bids must be in the US Dollar.
Bids must be submitted in person at the front counter of the Division of Natural Resources Development.
Outer envelopes must not contain identifying information about the bidder.
Bids must be accompanied by a deposit of 5% of the bid amount, submitted in the form of a certified check (cashier&apos;s check) or other verified funds.
Deposits for unsuccessful bidders may be picked up in person or destroyed, at the bidder&apos;s choice.
For the successful bidder, the deposit will be applied toward the down payment.
Successful bidders who choose not to purchase the property, forfeit the deposit.
Successful bidders who chose to purchase will need to sign a land Purchase and Sale Agreement and provide an additional 5% deposit, for a total down payment of 10% of the purchase price.
We encourage you to find a real estate agent or buyer representative from a State of Alaska Licensee and work with the closing agency (title company) of your choice.
Over-The-Counter Sale
Properties that do not first sell in auction, become available for over-the-counter sale and may be purchased on a first-come, first-served basis at the minimum auction price.
23 Lots within the West Chatanika River Recreational subdivision are available for over-the-counter sale! Visit the West Chatanika River Recreation Sale Page for more information.
Direct Sale
Some Borough-lands are available for sale year-round to those who meet specific direct sale land interest qualifications. Read more about the qualifications below and use our interactive lands map to find Borough lands near you.
Qualifications
Borough land may be eligible for purchase through direct sale if you meet any of the following qualifications:
You have had a lease, mining claim, or easement on the Borough parcel for at least 4 years.
You have had a lease, mining claim, or easement on a parcel next to the Borough parcel for at least 4 years.
You own land next to the Borough parcel.
Conditions
Borough land that is larger than 40 acres would need to be subdivided and platted.
Sale price will be 10% above Fair Market Value, to be determined by independent appraisal.
If more than one person qualifies and is interested in purchasing, a sealed bid auction will decide the buyer.
Buyer is responsible for the cost of appraisal, surveying, and platting as needed.
Tax Foreclosure
The FNSB Financial Services Treasury and Budget Division may hold an auction for properties with unpaid taxes. These properties and the auction timelines will be listed on the Treasury and Budget Tax Foreclosure page . For questions about Tax Foreclosure properties, please contact the Treasury and Budget Division.
Land Sale Listings
The Borough has 23 lots within the West Chatanika River Recreational subdivision available for over-the-counter sale. Visit the West Chatanika River Recreation sale page for more information.
