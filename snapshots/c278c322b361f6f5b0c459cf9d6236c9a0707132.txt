Notice to Bidders
All Sales Are Final
Obtain a Bidder Certificate stating that all taxes have been paid from the Smith County Tax Office. It&apos;s good for 90 days.
Anyone may bid on the Tax Foreclosed properties by registering at least five business days before the auction.
All sales are subject to the right of redemption by the owners, or any person having an interest therein, to redeem said property within the time and manner provided by law.
Payment is due by 4 pm CT on the day of the sale. The winning bidder&apos;s deposit will be applied first.
A tax sale deed or a tax resale deed to the property will be processed within 90 days. It may take longer during the holidays.
For more information on tax sales, you may contact the Linebarger law firm online or by calling 903-597-2897, or the Perdue law firm online or by calling 903-597-7664. Call the Smith County Civil Division at 903-520-1037.
