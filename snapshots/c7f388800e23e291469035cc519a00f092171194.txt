Tax Sale
About the Sale
The annual Black Hawk County Tax Sale is held on the third Monday in June in the IT Conference Room, Room 204, 316 East 5th Street. The sale begins at 8 a.m. and ends at 3:30 p.m. each day until all delinquent taxes have been offered for sale.
Unsold Items
Any Regular Tax Sale delinquencies that are offered at the annual Tax Sale but remain unsold will be offered at an Adjourned Tax Sale, which will be held in the Treasurer's office, beginning at 8 a.m. on the 3rd Monday of each month until all delinquencies have been sold.

Redeem Your Sale
To redeem a tax sale, guaranteed funds are required such as cash, certified or cashiers check or money order.

All delinquencies as of May 1 will be published in the Waterloo Cedar Falls Courier.
