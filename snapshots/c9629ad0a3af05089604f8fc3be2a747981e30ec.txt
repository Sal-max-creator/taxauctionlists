Tax Sale Information
The tax sale will be held November 5, 2026 and begin promptly at 2:00 pm. The sale will be held in Room 117 at the Whiteside County Courthouse, Morrison, IL 61270. We will be conducting the sale using R.A.M.S 2. If you are unfamiliar with this process, please visit www.ramsacutions.com/RAMS@.html for more information. Pursuant to Public Act 104-0553, for the 2025 payable 2026 tax sale, all real estate tax certificates will be acquired by the County, As Trustee.
The purpose of the annual tax sale is to collect delinquent real estate taxes for the benefit of all taxing districts and to restore tax delinquent property to tax paying status. Please be aware that when you purchase at the tax sale, you are purchasing real estate taxes and not property. Purchasing taxes is a complex legal procedure and we highly recommend that you contact an attorney, and review the Illinois Compiled State Statutes, Chapter 35.
You must register in writing 10 business days prior to the tax sale with the Whiteside County Collector's office. There is a $500 deposit in United States funds due with your registration form and that money will be applied to your purchases at the sale. Whoever signs the registration form must be physically present at the sale to bid. The sale is automated and facilitated by : RAMS Auctions, Joseph E Meyer & Associates Inc, 141 St Andrews Ave, Edwardsville IL 62025 ( 1-800-248-2850) or ( 1-616-656-5744).
A delinquent tax sale list will be available in the Collector's office. There is a $100 non-refundable fee to purchase the list. Please note: the tax sale list is provided for the sole purpose of the delinquent property tax sale. Use of this information for any other purpose is strictly prohibited. The County Collector reserves the right to deny sale of the list to anyone violating this rule. Delinquent tax sale listings are also published in part in local newspapers.
