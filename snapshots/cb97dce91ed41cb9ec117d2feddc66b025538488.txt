Delinquent Tax Sale
Each year, the Scott County Clerk holds a sale of the previous year&#8217;s tax bills that remain unpaid. Anyone interested in acquiring a delinquent tax bill must register with the Scott County Clerk to become, what is known as, a &#8216;third-party purchaser&#8217; and participate in the sale. Learn more about participation requirements and regulations at the Department of Revenue website.
Read below to find out more about the Scott County Clerk Delinquent Tax Sale and how to register with us!
