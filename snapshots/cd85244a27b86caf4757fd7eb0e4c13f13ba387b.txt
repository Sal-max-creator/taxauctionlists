Tax Collector
The Municipality of Oakland announces the sale of 2026 and prior year delinquent t axes and other municipal charges through an online auction on a date TBD.
For a listing of parcels, please visit https://oakland.newjerseytaxsale.com Information can be viewed free of charge on a date TBD.
FAILURE TO RECEIVE A TAX BILL DOES NOT RELIEVE THE OBLIGATION OF THE TAX PAYER.
Payments:
For your convenience we have a drop box on the left hand side of the Borough Hall Front Door. Any payments dropped off after 4:30 pm will be posted the next business day.
You can set up tax payments to be automatically withdrawn from your bank account by submitting an ACH Authorization form to the Finance Department (1 Municipal Plaza, Oakland, NJ 07436).
To avoid statutory interest, tax payments must be received in the Tax Office by the 10th day of the month when a quarter is due. If the 10th falls on a holiday or weekend, grace period will be extended to the following business day. After that, the interest is retroactive to the 1st of the month in which the payment is due. POSTMARK IS NOT ACCEPTABLE.
Interest:
Any delinquency is subject to interest at the rate of 8% per annum on the first $ 1,500 and 18% per annum for any amount over $ 1,500 retroactive to the first of the month. There is also a 6% year end penalty applied December 31st on any balances over $ 10,000.
Tax Payments Due Dates:
Quarterly payments become due on August 1st, November 1st, February 1st and May 1st.
Receipts:
Your canceled check is your receipt. If you do require a printed receipt, please send us a stamped, self-addressed envelope, and a receipt will be mailed to you.
Returned Checks:
If your check is returned to us you will be charged a $20 handling fee.
Tax Sale:
In New Jersey, all municipalities are required to hold at least one tax sale per year. The properties included in the tax sale are those with delinquent property taxes and/or municipal charges that are unpaid on the 11th day of the 11th month of the current tax year. (Pursuant to N J. S A. 54:5-19).
The Borough of Oakland will hold its annual online accelerated tax lien sale in December 2026. For the listing of parcels included in this year&apos;s tax sale visit https://oakland.newjerseytaxsale.com .
The statute requires a Public Notice of the Tax Sale. All delinquencies as of 11/11 of the current year will appear in the Public Notice.
Any properties whose account is made current by December 17, 2026 will be removed from the Tax Sale. The amount owed is calculated as follows:
Total Amount Owed = Delinquent Taxes and/or other Municipal Charges + Interest + Cost of Sale (2% of delinquent up to $100).
To receive the exact amount owed and any questions should be directed to the Tax Collector, Anisa K. Mainiero at 201-337-8111, ext. 2010, or [email&#160;protected]
If a tax lien has been purchased against your property and you would like to make payment to redeem the lien, please complete the Tax Title Lien Redemption Request and return it, with applicable payment, to the Tax Collector at 1 Municipal Plaza, Oakland, NJ 07436.
All payments received after November 10 MUST be made using guaranteed funds: cash or cashier&apos;s check. Any other forms of payment WILL NOT be accepted. All other checks WILL be returned.
Note: Failure of the property owner to receive a notice of tax sale properly mailed by the Tax Collector shall not constitute grounds to void the subsequent tax sale.
Tax Appeals and Property Tax Relief Programs:
Tax appeal forms are available and must be filed with the County Board of Taxation by April 1st of the current year at One Bergen County Plaza, 3rd Floor, Hackensack, NJ 07601. Please visit the Board of Taxation website or call 201-336-6300.
View Division of Taxation Property Tax Relief Program
In accordance with the provisions of N J. S A. 54:4-64, the first half taxes of any year are on the basis of half the taxes of the preceding year.
WHEN OWNERSHIP OF THE PROPERTY CHANGES, THE TAX BILL IS TO BE FORWARDED TO THE NEW OWNER(S) OR THEIR PAYING AGENT.
