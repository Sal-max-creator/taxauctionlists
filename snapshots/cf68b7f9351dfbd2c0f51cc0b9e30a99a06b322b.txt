If you have questions about billing and payment , please reach out to the Tax Collector&apos;s Office. For questions about assessment , or if you want to get a motor vehicle tax bill adjusted , reach out to the Tax Assessor&apos;s Office.
If you want to pay your property tax bill while our office is closed, these are your options :
Mail your payment (see below)
Pay online (click orange button above)
Pay by phone by calling 877 403 0227 (new number, as of June 15, 2026) . T his is an automated system. If you call this number to pay, your "year" for currently due bills (July 2026) is entered as "2025" because you are paying on the &apos;grand list&apos; of 2025 . Have your bill in front of you when you call. You will need the last seven digits of your BILL NUMBER. For example: If this number appears online: 2025-03-0352327 : 2025 is the year ; 03 is the type of tax (motor vehicle); 0352327 is the bill number. PAYING ONLINE:
You have several options when paying online. You may pay using an electronic check ("E check" / ACH payment). T here is NO ADDITIONAL FEE for using an E Check . Many taxpayers find this an economical alternative to using a credit or debit card, where 2.25% is charged as a fee. E Check provides a secure, worry-free, delay-free and paperless transaction.
You may also use a credit, debit or ATM card online . The non-refundable fee for using a credit, debit, or ATM card online is an additional 2.25% with a $2.00 minimum , paid to the service provider, Velocity.
These services are available at any time, 24/7. We hope that offering the E-Check option at no additional fee encourages taxpayers to opt for a secure, delay-free, and PAPERLESS transaction, in keeping with Norwalk&apos; quest to be the Greenest City!
PAYING OVER THE PHONE:
You may call 1-877-403-0227 to pay by phone. This is an automated system. If you call this number to pay, your "year" for current bills is "2025" (grand list of 2025). Have your bill in front of you when you call - you will need your BILL NUMBER, found on your bill, or online. For example: If this number appears online: 2025-03-0352327 :
2025 is the year ; 03 is the type of tax (motor vehicle); 0352327 is the bill number.
You may use a debit, credit, or ATM card, OR AN E CHECK, to pay over the phone .
The pay by phone service is automated. However, if you wish to have a LIVE AGENT assist with your transaction, you have the option of having a Personal Teller assist. You will pay an additional $3.95 teller fee to the service provider for this service.
IN TEREST:
Interest is charged on late payments at the rate of 1.5% per month, 18% per year, from the due date of the tax. Please note that the due date is July 1 or January 1. Interest reverts back to the July 1 or January 1 due date, NOT the last day to pay (August 1 or February 1).
Interest is charged by the month, not by the day. The minimum interest charge is $2 on each installment. You have a month&apos;s grace period to pay without interest.
Liens were filed on April 1, 2026, and there is now an additional $24 charge to release the lien.

Looking Up Your Taxes Online:
We maintain five years of payment history online on the website. Our website is updated daily.
When looking up your taxes on our website, select the option to view "ALL" if you want to see all bills, including prior years. You can choose "DUE NOW" if you want to see only your current bill(s).
You can search by name, property location, parcel number (for real estate) or other variables. We recommend you search by your last name and first initial. You are able to put items directly in your "shopping cart" to pay them.

Mill rates and operating budget synopsis FYE 2027
Assessments for the 2025 Grand List are as of October 1, 2025. Real estate assessments for this grand list will reflect Year #3 of the four year phase in of the 2023 citywide property revaluation.
Revaluation Phase-in: July 2026 & January 2027 is Year 3 of 4
On February 29, 2024, the City of Norwalk finalized a revaluation of all taxable property. By state statute, this was required to be a full physical revaluation. The results were reflective of a strong, post-pandemic residential housing market and a relatively flat to down commercial market. Overall, values were up an average of 35% citywide.
After a lengthy and detailed analysis, the Mayor and City Boards made the decision to phase in the revaluation to mitigate the impact of the tax increases to residential property owners. This phase in began in July 2024, and will take place over four fiscal years.
Tax bills sent in July 2024 and January 2025 reflected the FIRST year of this four year process.
Tax bills issued in July 2025 and January 2026 reflected the SECOND year of the four year process.
Tax bills sent in July 2026 and January 2027 reflect the THIRD year of the four year process .
Tax bills sent in July 2027 and January 2028 will reflect the FOURTH, and final, year of the process.

FRAUD ALERT UPDATE: BE INFORMED! Tax payment checks can be STOLEN from the mailstream.
The City of Norwalk Tax Collector's office, and Norwalk taxpayers, have experienced situations where taxpayers have mailed checks to pay their property taxes, but the tax collector's office never receives their checks. The checks are stolen from the mail stream and cashed by fraudsters. Taxpayers ultimately become aware that their checks were not timely received by the City, and reach out to the tax collector's office expecting to be able to pay without interest.
Taxpayers should be aware that interest is charged on late payments regardless of the reason. Even if a taxpayer is victim to this type of mail fraud, the tax collector does not have the authority to accept their payment without interest. Furthermore, there is no other official at City Hall, or at the State, with this authority. The Connecticut Tax Collectors&apos; Association has for the last several years proposed changing the law in Connecticut to allow victims of this type of fraud to have their late payment interest waived in cases where the fraud can be documented, but to date, the law has not been changed.
Click the link here for more information about "check washing," mail fraud, and links to articles about what you can do to avoid becoming a victim of mail fraud: Checks Stolen / Mail Fraud

Responsibilities:
The tax collector&apos;s office is responsible for the billing and collection of property taxes and other charges that comprise approximately 90% of the city&apos;s operating revenue. The office operates under the authority of the Connecticut General Statutes .
Taxes and charges collected include:
Real estate taxes
Motor vehicle taxes
Business personal property taxes
Sewer use fees
Sewer assessment fees, IPP fees, and other city charges
