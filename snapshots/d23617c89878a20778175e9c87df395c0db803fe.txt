2026 Tax Sale Date
The 2026 Tax Sale is scheduled for Thursday, December 10, 2026, at 10 am, at 140 Eagle Rock Avenue, Roseland, NJ 07068, on Level 2A. All bidders must be registered by December 9, 2026. Please complete the Tax Sale Bidder Information Sheet and W-9 form, and submit them to Maureen Chumacas by 4:30 pm on December 9, 2026. There will be no same-day registration.
You will receive a bidder number through email. You must have a bidder number to bid on any property at the tax sale.
Contact Maureen Chumacas .
What is a Tax Sale?
A tax sale is held each year for non-payment of prior year(s) taxes, assessments, and other municipal charges. The tax sale is advertised in the Caldwell Progress and posted on our Borough website.
State law regarding the New Jersey tax sale process can be found in New Jersey statute: NJ.S.A. 54:5-19, etc. View the New Jersey statutes.
Read more about the elements of Tax Sale in New Jersey.
All payments at tax sale must be made with certified funds, cash, money orders, or by wire transfer. Time is given to go to the bank. Separate checks are needed for Tax, Utility, and Premiums.
We urge you to know the tax sale procedure and research any property you bid on.
Responsibilities of a Successful Lien Holder
The purchased Tax Sale Certificate must be recorded in the Essex County Clerk&apos;s Office within 90 days of the date of the tax sale.
The Tax Sale Certificate shall be issued to the successful bidder within ten days after the tax sale. After the receipt of the Certificate, the lien holder may check with the tax office for the amount due on delinquent current taxes or water/sewer billings.
An affidavit must be filed in the Tax Collector&apos;s Office when paying subsequent charges, in accordance with NJ.S.A. 54:5-60.
