Tax Commissioner
The Tift County Tax Commissioner's office is pleased to provide the citizens of Tift County with a site that provides information, payment options, and FAQ about property tax, as well as motor vehicle tax. Our site also offers convenient online payments of property tax and motor vehicle renewals. We strive to keep educated on the ever changing laws that affect our office. The Tift County Tax Commissioner's office collects and disburses all taxes collected to the state, county, city and board of education. My office staff and I are pleased to serve the citizens of Tift County.
