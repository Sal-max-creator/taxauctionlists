Mission Statement
We strive to serve the public in a helpful, courteous and efficient manner, while maintaining the utmost integrity and fairness. The customers of the Hall County Treasurer's Office are the purpose of our work.
To reach our mission these goals have to be accomplished:
Prompt and courteous service to the customer
Honesty
Maintain communication and cooperation with fellow employees, agencies and the public
Teamwork
