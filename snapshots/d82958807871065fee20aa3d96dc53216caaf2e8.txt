Public Notice: Tax Sale
October 28, 2026 by Riverton Borough
The BOROUGH OF RIVERTON announces the sale of 2025 and prior year delinquent taxes and other municipal charges through an on-line auction on Wednesday, December 2, 2026 – 8:30 am. For a listing of all parcels, delinquencies and costs, along with bidding instructions, please visit
https://riverton.newjerseytaxsale.com
**information can be viewed free of charge**
Please note: Check back for the parcel list.
