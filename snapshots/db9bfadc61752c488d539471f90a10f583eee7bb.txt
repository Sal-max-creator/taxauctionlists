Tax Deed Surplus Information
After the tax deed sale is complete, the clerk determines who may be entitled to any surplus funds. Claims must be ﬁled within 120 days of the date of the surplus notice, except for property owner claims. After 120 days, information regarding disbursement of claims can be directed to our board ﬁnance clerk, Michelle Nolind, by phone (850) 747-5729 or by email to [email&#160;protected] . This process is managed by a private attorney. The law offices of Travis R Walker, P.A. can be contacted for more information about surplus funds by visiting traviswalkerlaw.com or calling (772) 708-0952 .
