Foreclosed Property
Auction
Title Check, LLC, handles the auction process for Manistee County. The auction for foreclosed properties is held once each year, typically in August or September, and is online only. The 2026 auction schedule is as follows:
Thursday, August 27, 2026 - Minimum bid auction (10:00 AM to 7:00 PM)
Thursday, August 27, 2026 - Winning bidders notified (approximately 8:00 PM)
Friday, September 25, 2026 - Re-offer auction at minimum bid (if any) (Sealed bids due by 7:00 PM)
Monday, September 28, 2026 - Re-offer auction bids awarded
Friday, October 30, 2026 - No reserve auction (if any) (Sealed bids due by 7:00 PM)
Monday, November 2, 2026 - No reserve auction bids awarded
View Title Check&apos;s FAQ for information on how to bid, who can bid, and other frequently asked questions.
