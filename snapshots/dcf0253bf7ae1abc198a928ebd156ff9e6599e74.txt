Treasurer
2025 Property Tax Bills were mailed early May, 2026. Installments were due on June 12th and September 11th, 2026.
Certified notices were mailed on October 23rd with final dates and amounts listed, correct amounts due will be on our website as well. Penalties change every 30 days - Last Day payments can be accepted is Friday, December 4th to avoid tax sale!
Please Note : THE PO BOX WAS CLOSED IN 2024 - Please update your bill payment information for your property tax payments with your online banking.
Please use our physical address to mail your payments: 404 Elm Street, Rm 205, Rockford, IL 61101
Tax Bills are available online and we are accepting payments in our office, online and over the phone @ 815-859-9650 with debit & credit cards (please note there is a charge of 2.25% with all cards) and check payments are free. Same fees applied to payments made directly in our office. When taxes are not paid by the due date a penalty is attached of 1.5% per month per installment. The last day to make a property tax payment will be Friday, December 4th if you have no prior year delinquent taxes. Payments must be in our office or paid online by this date - postmarks will NOT be accepted this last day of payments. If taxes are not paid they will go into tax sale and paid by a tax buyer with a high interest attached. You will have limited time to redeem (payback) these taxes to avoid loss of your property.
To make a payment online click the VIEW or PAY BILL Link in the MENU box below or call 815-859-9650 to pay over the phone - the link below and phone payment option have correct amounts with penalties listed.
Parking is VERY limited due to construction on court house.
Online / phone payments are recommended due to these limitations.
If you have not paid your taxes for 2024 or prior years you will need to contact the Winnebago County Clerks office at 815-319-4253, prior year / back taxes CANNOT be paid online . A tax buyer paid your taxes for you and attached a percentage rate. You will have a period of 2.5 years to redeem your taxes; 1 year for vacant non-farm land or properties with 7 or more residential units or commercial / industrial properties. Period of redemption may be extended by the certificate holder. Within the 2.5-year period the interest rate will increase every six months. It is in your best interest to redeem (pay back) your taxes as soon as possible. If a tax buyer has paid 2024 taxes due to not redeeming prior years ( ex: Paid by states Subsequent - Name) you will need to contact the Winnebago County Clerks Office at 815-319-4253 to redeem your taxes. See 35 ILCS 200/21-350 for complete statutes.
The purpose of the Treasurer's Office is to assist taxpayers of Winnebago County, collect funds and distribute to the taxing districts throughout the year. The office has many other duties outside of property and mobile taxes. The Treasurer's Office is also responsible for: investment of county funds, balance bank statements, record and manage bankruptcies, enter owner information for newly recorded deeds, change addresses for taxpayers, process tax bill corrections received from the Supervisor of Assessments office, mail refunds, receive and record deposits daily, issue mobile home certifications, collect and deposit Hotel/Motel tax fees. Additionally, we accept payments for miscellaneous county fines, as well as register, process, call and balance our annual tax sale. Theresa Grennan has been employed in the Treasurer's Office since 1994. She was formerly the Chief Deputy Treasurer and appointed Treasurer on January 31, 2025. Theresa and her staff are available to assist taxpayers with any problems or issues they may have with their taxes in person, by phone or via email during office hours.
