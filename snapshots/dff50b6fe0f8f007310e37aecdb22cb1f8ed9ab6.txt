The Henry County Tax Commissioners Office holds judicial-in-rem tax sales on the first Tuesday of the month. These tax sales are held promtly at 10:00 am at: 140 Henry Parkway McDonough Ga 30253.
Please allow time for the registration and verification process
REGISTRATION:
Bidders must register prior to the day of tax online.
Bidders must complete the registration and have funds verified in person the morning of the tax sale ( with their acceptable forms of payment in hand ) by visiting the Property Tax Department with the Henry County Tax Commissioners office located at 140 Henry Pkwy McDonough. GA 30253
REQUIREMENTS:
Provide driver license as photo ID, address and phone number.
Verification of funds ( actual funds in hand ) by the a Property Tax Specialist at the Henry County Tax Commissioner&apos;s Office located at 140 Henry Pkwy McDonough, GA 30253
The only acceptable forms of payment are cash, money order and certified checks, payable to Henry County Tax Commissioner or HCTC. NO PERSONAL CHECKS OR BANKS STATMENTS
Must provide separate payment for each property purchased at tax sale. Multiple checks can be accepted for each individual property, but checks cannot be split between two parcels.
Allow extra time for verification of cash.
Once funds are verified, bidders will be given a bidding number.
Only registered bidders will be allowed o participate in the bidding process.
Registered bidders will not be allowed to bid more than the amount of funds verified.
Tax bidders cannot bid on properties they or their corporation/ LLC own.
Payment is required at the time of the sale and sales are final.
