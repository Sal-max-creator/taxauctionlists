Auditor
Thank you for your interest in your local government. The auditor&apos;s office is committed to providing professional and courteous service in a timely manner, maintaining accurate accounting and reporting, and following federal, state, and local laws in an efficient and cost-effective manner.
Welcome to the Tippecanoe County Auditor&apos;s Office.
The auditor is the Chief Financial Officer of county government, covering a broad set of duties related to county finances, property tax administration, and records management. Contact the office by calling 765.423.9207 or emailing auditor@tippecanoe.in.gov .
Responsibilities
Completing annual reports for audit by the State Board of Accounts.
Preparing monthly financial statements and balancing with the County Treasurer.
Calculating property tax for all real, personal, mobile home, drainage, and special assessment records.
Distributing tax collections, special assessments, and special revenue collections for all units of government within Tippecanoe County, including the cities of Lafayette and West Lafayette, towns, townships, schools, libraries, etc.
Receipting and disbursing funds, including auditing accounts payable
Recording and depreciating capital assets
Processing county employee payroll
Compiling the annual budget
Handling all transfers of ownership of real property
Processing property tax deductions
Tracking Tax Increment Finance (TIF) areas
Maintaining property boundaries and parcel layer information on GIS
Calculating property tax for all real, personal, mobile home, drainage, and special assessment records
The Auditor also serves as secretary to the county executive and fiscal body, and prepares meeting minutes, records votes, and maintains various documents of public record.
