ABOUT US
Fayette County Clerk: A Legacy of Service
The Fayette County Clerk’s Office is a state constitutional office with duties governed by the Kentucky Revised Statutes. The Clerk is responsible for the recording and administration of official documents, preparation of property tax bills, registration of voters and conduct of elections, and registration and titling of motor vehicles.
Fayette County is one of three original counties formed in 1780, and our land records date back to before Kentucky’s statehood in 1792. Under the leadership of Susan Lamb, the office remains dedicated to serving the community with integrity while honoring our historical legacy.

Susan Lamb
Fayette County Clerk

Years Experience
Susan Lamb has been a dedicated public servant in Lexington-Fayette County since 1986. Over the years, she worked in several key roles, including:
27 years with the Lexington-Fayette Urban County Government, 8 years (4 terms) on Lexington-Fayette Urban County Council and now serving as the Fayette County Clerk
In 2023, Susan made her mark in history as Lexington&#8217;s first female County Clerk. She is committed to serving the community with integrity, transparency, and a focus on inclusivity. Susan looks forward to continuing to serve Fayette County with dedication and pride.

Quick Help
Where can I find vehicle forms?
You will find vehicle forms here: Forms
I didn’t receive my renewal notice. Where can I find it?
The renewal notice is not necessary for renewal and is considered a courtesy notice sent by the Transportation Cabinet. For the cost of the renewal please call the office.
I paid off my vehicle, how do I release my lien?
To terminate your lien, please mail or bring the lien release statement and your vehicle title to the office. Your lien will be released in our database and marked terminated on the title and returned to you.
How can I renew my vehicle registration online?
To renew online your address must be current, your registration must not be expired and you must have verifiable proof of insurance. If you meet all requirements you can go to https://secure.kentucky.gov/kytc/renewal
What do I need to transfer a vehicle?
Original Vehicle title, KY proof of insurance and ID are required. Vehicles purchased or moving from another state will require a sheriff’s inspection. Please see Vehicles for more specific information.
