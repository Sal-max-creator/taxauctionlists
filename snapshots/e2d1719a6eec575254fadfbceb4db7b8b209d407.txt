About Isabella County
Located in the heart of Central Lower Michigan, Isabella County was once one of the most magnificent pine and hardwood areas in all of the Great Lakes region. It was part of the Indian's winter hunting grounds and was called "Ojibiway Besse" (the place of the Chippewa). Isabella County is proud to be home to Central Michigan University, the Saginaw Chippewa Indian Tribe and the City of Mount Pleasant. Learn more about Isabella County ›
