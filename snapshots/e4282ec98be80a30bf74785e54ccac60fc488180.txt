Tax Department Duties & Responsibilities
Collecting all property and mobile home taxes, special assessments, and drainage tax
Collecting miscellaneous revenue from other offices
Investing all county funds
Apportioning all tax dollars and sending to appropriate school, city, or other taxing body
Issuing tax clearances on mobile homes
Processing Property Tax Credit Claims for senior citizens or persons with disabilities
Property Tax Credit
Mobile Home Tax Credit
Keeping accurate records of all monies received and disbursed
Holding the annual Tax Sale

Tax Timeline
July
Beginning of fiscal year.
August
Fiscal year property taxes are certified.
Mailing of property and mobile home taxes, special assessments and drainage taxes.
September
First half property taxes due September 1; delinquent after September 30, unless that day falls on Saturday or Sunday, then delinquent after first business day in October.
October
First half property taxes delinquent as of October 1, unless September 30 deadline was a Saturday or Sunday, then taxes not delinquent until 2nd business day in October. Interest accrues at the rate of 1.5% per month (rounded to nearest dollar - $1 minimum).
November
Delinquent notices are mailed to property owners with outstanding September taxes.
November 15 - Tax sale investors may begin paying subsequent delinquent taxes on outstanding tax sale certificates.
December
January
Elderly & Disabled Tax Credit and Rent Reimbursement forms become available. Individuals can apply between January 1 and June 1.
February
March
Second half property taxes due March 1, delinquent after March 31, unless that day falls on Saturday or Sunday, then delinquent after first business day in April.
April
Second half property taxes delinquent as of April 1, unless March 31 deadline was a Saturday or Sunday, then taxes not delinquent until 2nd business day in April. Interest accrues at the rate of 1.5% per month (rounded to nearest dollar - $1 minimum).
May
May 1 - Tax Sale Notices are mailed to owners with outstanding taxes.
May 15 - Tax sale investors may begin paying subsequent delinquent taxes on outstanding tax sale certificates.
June
June 1 - Elderly & Disabled Tax Credit Claims are due, unless County Treasurer has extended deadline until September 30.
3rd Monday - Annual Tax Sale is held at 10:00 a.m.
