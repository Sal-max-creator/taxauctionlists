Annual Real Estate and Mobile Home Tax Sale&#160;
Fall Date of Sale & Registration
Pursuant to Public Act 104-0553, the McLean County Board, as trustee for the taxing districts, has elected to acquire all delinquent real estate taxes for 2025 payable in 2026 taxes. Delinquent Mobile Home taxes will continue to be offered at the tax sale to private tax buyers under the applicable provisions of Illinois law.
The annual tax sale will be held on November 6, 2026. Sale begins at 10:00 am and all 2026 delinquent mobile home taxes are offered for sale, both separately and in consecutive order. Anyone can register to purchase taxes at the sale, but they must register to be a buyer at least ten business days prior to the sale. Click here for the annual delinquent mobile home tax sale registration form.
