Foreclosed Property Sales
2026 Tax Foreclosed Properties
Properties foreclosed upon for the non-payment of 2023 and/or prior years property taxes by the order of the 51st Circuit Court on April 1, 2026 will be offered at public auction.
AUCTION ROUND 2:
Properties that were not previously sold at either the Round 1 or the Follow-Up Round 1 Auctions will be offered at a no reserve public auction on Friday, October 30, 2026. All bids must be placed in advance. Bidding is available 10:00 am to 7:00 pm EDT on Friday, October 30, 2026. Winning bids will be awarded 12:00 pm Monday, November 2, 2026. For more information, please click the link below.
Listings and information are now available on the following website:
Online information: www.tax-sale.info
The above website is provided by Title-Check LLC, the company who conducts the auction, and provides sale information and sale conditions. Information about the properties to be sold will appear at the above website.
Notice of Intention to Claim Interest in Foreclosure Sales Proceeds
Beginning with 2021 foreclosure sales and transfers, a person that intends to make a claim for excess sales proceeds must complete and return the above form after notarization to the Mason County Treasurer's Office by July 1 in the year of foreclosure. This notice must be delivered via certified mail, return receipt requested, or by personal service. Completing and returning this form evidences intent to make a future claim but is not itself a claim for sales proceeds. If you have any questions regarding this form, please contact our office.
As in prior years, the Mason County Treasurer has arranged for tax-foreclosed properties to be sold at an online auction conducted by Title-Check LLC. Specific information concerning the sale, including a list of properties, may be obtained by contacting:
