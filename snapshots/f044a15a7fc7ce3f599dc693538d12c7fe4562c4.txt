IMPORTANT – PLEASE READ
The 2026–2027 Secured Tax Roll is now available.
You can now search for and pay secured, unsecured, defaulted, supplemental, and escape bills.
Click Continue to begin. Follow the prompts to search, select, and pay your bill.
Your payment is not complete until you click “Submit” and receive a “Successful” receipt.
How to Search
• Secured/defaulted/supplemental/escape: Parcel number or current mailing address
• Unsecured (businesses, boats, aircraft): 4‑digit year + 6‑digit bill number
Outside the U.S.?
If you encounter access errors, your country may be blocked. Please:
• Mail payment (postmarked by the delinquent date), or
• Pay by phone: (855) 829‑3773
Duplicate Bills
You may purchase duplicate bills for $1.00 at any Tax Collector’s office . Branch offices accept checks, cashier’s checks, and money orders .
