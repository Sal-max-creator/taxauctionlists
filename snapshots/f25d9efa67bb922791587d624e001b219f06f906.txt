Annual Tax Auctions
NOTICE TO ALL TAX BUYERS: Effective January 2026, Jefferson County has adopted the " Single Bidder Rule " for all future tax sales.
NOTICE TO ALL TAX BUYERS: Pursuant to Public Act 104-0553 , Jefferson County has elected to acquire all delinquent real estate property taxes as trustee for the taxing districts beginning with the 2025 PAYABLE IN 2026 tax sale.
Delinquent mobile home taxes will continue to be offered at the tax sale to private tax buyers under the applicable provisions of Illinois law.
The Annual Sealed Bid Auction offers citizens the opportunity to submit bids for properties that have been foreclosed due to outstanding Real Estate Taxes.
Catalogs for the 2026 Sealed Bid Auction will be available in October.
Starting in 2023, bids may be placed online - call 618-656-5744 or visit iltaxsale.com for details.
To purchase a catalog of the properties, click here .
