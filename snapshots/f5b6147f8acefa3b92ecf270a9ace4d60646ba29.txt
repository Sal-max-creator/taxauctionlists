Tax Sale Information | Summit County Auditor, Utah
Notices
2026 Tax Sale Public Notice_5-19
Tax Sale Frequency
Any property not redeemed by March 15 following the lapse of four years from the date when the property became delinquent, will be included in the annual real property Tax Sale held in May or June.
Property List
The list of delinquent properties will be available online beginning four weeks before the Sale. The list will be periodically updated up to the day of the sale. Click HERE for the 2026 Summit County Notice of Tax Sale.
Newspaper Publication
The Tax Sale is advertised in the Park Record and the Summit News four times, once in each of the four successive weeks immediately preceding the date of the Tax Sale.
Location
The 2026 Summit County Tax Sale will be held online.
You may register as a bidder for the tax sale by visiting this link: https://www.publicsurplus.com/sms/summitcore,ut/list/current?orgid=973661 .
Date: May 21, 2026 | 10:00 a.m. - 3:00 p.m.
Anchor Location: 60 N. Main Street Coalville, UT (Conference Room 1)
Unsold Properties
Any property unsold at the Tax Sale, which is not in the public interest to be re-certified to a subsequent sale, shall become county property.
Properties Sold for More Than the Amount Due
Any sale funds in excess of the total amount of delinquent taxes, penalties, interest, and administrative fees will be treated as Unclaimed Property under Title 67 of the Utah Code Annotated and forwarded to the Utah State Treasurer after one year has elapsed from the date of the tax sale. Visit the website: mycash.utah.gov for more information.
