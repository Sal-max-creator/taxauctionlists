Hartford to Host Tax Sale Monday, June 26th
No longer on display. Expired on
June 27, 2023, 12:00 AM
On Monday, June 26th, the City of Hartford will host a tax sale for properties across the city.
The public auction will take place on Monday, June 26th, 2023 at 9 AM at Dunkin' Park, 1214 Main Street, Hartford CT. Registration opens at 9:00 AM.
The full list of properties as well as the starting bids is available at www.cttaxsales.com .
Bidders must present a deposit of $5,000 per property in certified funds payable to "Pullman & Comley, Trustee" and the winning bidder must pay the balance of the sale price within 5 days or forfeit that deposit. Bidders must be current with all property taxes owed to the City as bids will not be accepted from tax-delinquent bidders. More details are available at www.cttaxsales.com .
For questions regarding the tax sale please email the Hartford Tax office at hartfordtax@hartford.gov .
