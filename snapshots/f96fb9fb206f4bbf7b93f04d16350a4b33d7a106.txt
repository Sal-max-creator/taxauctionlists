2026 Property Tax Foreclosure Information
The Cowlitz County Treasurer's Office filed a Certificate of Delinquency with the Cowlitz County Superior Court on June 2, 2026, initiating foreclosure proceedings on 13 properties with unpaid property taxes from 2023 or earlier.
A list of properties currently included in the 2026 foreclosure process, along with the corresponding title reports, is available here. Links to individual title reports and GIS maps are also provided below the table.
Foreclosure Auction Information
The foreclosure auction is expected to be conducted online through PublicSurplus.com. Properties will be available for preview in late November.
You may review the Auction Terms and Conditions here.
The tentative auction date for all parcels, except for parcel number 080980081, is December 7, 2026 . The auction for parcel 080980081 will be scheduled separately at a later date.
Bidding is scheduled to open at 9:00 a.m. PST and close at 3:00 p.m. PST. Each parcel will be offered through a separate auction.
Auction details and updates, including the confirmed auction date for parcel 080980081, will be posted here as information becomes available.
Important Notice
Cowlitz County Treasurer's Office staff cannot provide legal advice regarding the foreclosure process. Individuals with questions or concerns about foreclosure proceedings are encouraged to seek advice from a qualified legal professional.
