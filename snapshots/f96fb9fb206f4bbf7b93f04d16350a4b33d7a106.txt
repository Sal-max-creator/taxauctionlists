2026 Foreclosure
The Cowlitz County Treasurer&apos;s Office filed a Certificate of Delinquency with Cowlitz County Superior Court on June 2, 2026, initiating foreclosure proceedings on 13 properties with unpaid property taxes from 2023 or earlier.
A list of the properties currently in the 2026 foreclosure process, along with the corresponding title reports is available here .
The foreclosure auction is expected to be held online through PublicSurplus.com .
The tentative auction date for all parcels, except parcel number 080980081 , is December 7, 2026. The auction for parcel 080980081 will be scheduled for a later date.
Auction information and updates, including the confirmed auction date for parcel 080980081, will be posted here as they become available.
