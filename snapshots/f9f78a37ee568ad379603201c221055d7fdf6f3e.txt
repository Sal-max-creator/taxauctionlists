The County Treasurer is responsible for overseeing the collection and distribution of over $484 million dollars to nearly 200 taxing bodies. This collection process extends over two installments and concludes with the annual tax sale. The McLean County Collector is billing over 68,000 real estate parcels. This website will provide you with the ability to confirm payment information for real estate property taxes. The web information does not detail who the payment was received from or if the taxes were paid as a result of being sold at the annual tax sale. If you have any questions or concerns about these matters, call the Treasurer's Office at (309) 888-5180.

Where Are Your Taxes Going?
