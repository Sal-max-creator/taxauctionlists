View & Pay Taxes Online
The Lafayette Parish Sheriff’s Office Web Tax Site offers the community the capability to view and/or pay their taxes online. You can pay your taxes with an E-Check, Visa, MasterCard, American Express, or Discover credit cards. A transaction fee will be applied for each option. Click on the link below to utilize this feature.
Please Note: Pop-up blockers must be temporarily disabled during usage.

Lafayette Parish Property Tax Sale FAQ
W-9 Form for Businesses
The annual City/Parish online delinquent property tax lien auction is set for Wednesday, June 10th, 2026 beginning at 9am and continuing through Thursday, June 11th, 2026 with the auction ending at 12 noon June 11th, 2026. Tax lien auction registration and online viewing of delinquent properties will be available starting 10am Monday, May 18th, 2026. The registration process will end 3pm Friday, June 5th, 2026. To register for the tax lien auction, the following link can be used: TAX AUCTION ONLINE or you may contact SGT. Ashley Ventroy at 337-236-5882; or Ashley.ventroy@lafayettesheriff.com for registration assistance.
All payments for tax lien certificates are due at end of auction Thursday, June 11th, 2026. Only form of payment accepted will be ACH Withdrawal.
For the City of Lafayette, tax lien auction payments for tax lien certificates are due at end of auction Thursday, June 11th, 2026. Only form of payment accepted will be ACH Withdrawal. The City of Lafayette Tax Office is located at 1875 W. Pinhook Rd Suite-B Lafayette, LA 70508. Their office number is 337-291-8272 and Rachel Hebert is the tax auction contact person. The office hours for the City of Lafayette’s Tax Office are 8:00am to 5:00pm Monday thru Friday.
The delinquent advertisement will run late in the local paper, The Acadiana Advocate on Sunday, May 24, 2026. For information as to what is a delinquent property tax lien auction, please refer to Revised Statue RS 47:2154. Property tax questions or tax lien auction questions can also be emailed to: Lafayette Parish Sheriff&#8217;s Office Tax Department
The Lafayette Parish Sheriff&#8217;s Office Tax Department is located at 1010 Lafayette Street, 4th Floor, Lafayette, LA 70501. The department’s phone number is (337) 236-5880 and the mailing address is LPSO Tax Department, P.O. Box 92590 Lafayette, LA 70509. The Tax Department’s office hours are 7:30am to 4:45pm Monday thru Thursday and 8:00am to 4:15pm Friday.
Payments for delinquent taxes will be accepted through the close of the business day Tuesday, June 9th, 2026. May 25th, 2026 through June 9th, 2026, Cash, Money Order or Certified Check will be the only forms of payment accepted for delinquent taxes. Online payments will be accepted until 4:30pm on June 5, 2026.
For questions concerning your tax assessment value, homestead exemption, or how your taxes are calculated, please visit The Lafayette Parish Tax Assessor&#8217;s Website or call (337) 291-7080.
