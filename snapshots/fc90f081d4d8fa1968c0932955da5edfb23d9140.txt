Delinquent Tax Sale List:
Property Listing & Advertising:
Property to be offered for sale will be advertised in the Seneca Journal Tribune for three consecutive weeks prior to the sale in the name of the defaulting taxpayer, defined by the South Carolina statutes as the owner of record on December 31st of the year preceding the taxable year.
Tax Sale List:
Properties on the Tax Sale List can be viewed below, downloaded and/or printed here , or viewed on this GIS Map .

1 Pursuant to the Code of Laws of South Carolina, the following real estate located in Oconee County, South Carolina will be sold at public auction to satisfy delinquent property taxes, penalties, assessments, and costs. The auction will be held at the Walhalla Performing Arts Center, 101 East North Broad Street, Walhalla, South Carolina, beginning at 10:00 am on Monday, November 9, 2026. All bidders must be registered to participate. Registration must be completed online or in person at the Oconee County Delinquent Tax Office prior to the day of sale. Registration will be open October 29, 2026, through November 5, 2026. Bidder registration will NOT be accepted on the day of sale. Bidders are responsible for conducting their own property research. Opening bid will include delinquent and current taxes, penalties, and applicable costs. Amounts listed are subject to change. Properties are listed alphabetically by the owner of record as of December 31, 2024. Ownership may have changed since that date. For bidder registration and additional information, please visit the Delinquent Tax webpage at www.oconeesc.com.
