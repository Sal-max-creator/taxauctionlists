Tax Deeds
Tax Deeds
Tax Deed Sales deal with the selling of property to the highest bidder at a public auction to recover delinquent property taxes. A tax deed sale occurs after an owner of a Tax Certificate applies to the Tax Collector for a Tax Deed after the Tax Certificate has been held for the statutory period of twenty-two [22] months from the date of delinquency.
A deputy clerk employed by the Clerk of the Circuit Court conducts the sale or public auction in accordance with Florida Statute 197.502[5] . Tax Deed Sales are held as scheduled and noticed at 11:00 AM on the front steps of the Liberty County Courthouse facing Hwy 20.
Certificate shall be SOLD to the highest bidder at the Courthouse door at 11:00 AM EST.
