2026 Notice to Tax Sale Purchaser
2026 Notice to Tax Sale List
Important Dates:
Sunday May 31, 2026 - Guaranteed funds are required for taxes
Friday May 15, 2026 - To avoid publication
Friday June 12, 2026 - In person Taxes must be paid by 4:00 pm
Sunday June 7th, 2026 - Online Payments accepted through 11:59 pm
Tax Sale Information:
The 2026 Webster County Tax Sale will begin at 8:00 a.m. on Monday, June 15, 2026. The Sale will be held at the Webster County Courthouse and bidders shall place their bids online . Bidders need not be present at the courthouse.
