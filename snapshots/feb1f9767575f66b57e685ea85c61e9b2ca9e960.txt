Online Vehicle Registration Renewal
To renew your vehicle registration online visit the State of Montana's website at http://www.mt.gov or go directly to the link to the application at Motor Vehicle Division
315 4th Street Havre, MT 59501
(406)265-5481 ext. 2419
Vehicle Title and Registration forms are available through the state of Montana, Department of Justice https://mvdmt.gov/forms-manuals-vehicle-title-registration .
Out of state residents who are temporarily working in Montana or are using their vehicle for hire, compensation or profit, must display Montana plates and pay taxes for using Montana public roadways. Because they are not Montana residents, they cannot title their vehicles in Montana. To qualify for a registration purposes only permit, the out of state resident must provide a current out of state registration. A temporary registration is acceptable, as long as it's current.
Students who are residents of another state do not need to plate and register their vehicle unless they are working while going to school. They must title if they own the vehicle, but a registration purpose only permit is allowed if the vehicle is registered to a different owner, parent for example.
It is unlawful to operate a motor vehicle in the State of Montana without valid vehicle liability insurance policy, certificate of self-insurance or posted indemnity.
I have moved to Montana, what do I need to register my car?
If you have recently moved to Montana and need to transfer your title, or if an out-of-state- party is holding the title for your vehicle, it is still possible to transfer the title to Montana.
Ask your County Treasurer for a Foreign Title Transfer (MV63) . Complete and sign the form.
Mail the form to the secured party, asking that your out-of-state title be released to your county treasurer's office.
When the county treasurer's office receives the secured party's request and the out-of-state title, you will then complete a title and registration in your local Montana county treasurers office. The New Montana title can then be sent to the out-of-state secured party if requested.
MT Motor Vehicle Division &ndash; Driver & Vehicle Services Made Easy (mvdmt.gov)
I may want personal plates..
Personalized plates are available for an additional fee starting at $39. If you want to personalize a sponsored plate, there are additional sponsor fees. For requirements and additional information, see the Application for Personalized License Plates (MV8) .
MT Motor Vehicle Division &ndash; Driver & Vehicle Services Made Easy (mvdmt.gov)
New registration needed, plates gone, decal missing?
Lost your registration? With a current registration we can reprint your registration card at no cost to you!
If a plate is lost, stolen, or damaged, a customer may come into the county treasurer's office and replace them. If they are a personalized plate, one will be re-ordered for you, and a temporary plate will be issued. Customer is responsible for the replacement plate fees.
Need a new decal? Come on in and we can get you a replacement with a decal replacement fee.
MT Motor Vehicle Division &ndash; Driver & Vehicle Services Made Easy (mvdmt.gov)
How much will it cost?
Registration costs are based on state established fee schedules. In addition to your registration fee, Hill County imposes a county option tax. This tax is based on the manufacturer's suggested retail price, depreciated according to the schedule established by law. https://dojmt.gov/driving/mvd-faqs/
When and where do I pay?
The Hill County Treasurer accepts cash, debit and credit cards, personal checks and money orders made out to Hill County Treasurer in the exact amount of your registration. Please do not send cash through the mail.

Heavy Vehicle Use Tax 2290
About Form 2290/Heavy highway vehicle tax return/ Internal Revenue Service
* 23 CFR 669.11 requires that the Governor of each state certify HVUT compliance prior to July 1 each year.
*Certifying that the state is obtaining proof of payment as a condition of registering heavy trucks subject to the HVUT.
*The state or other responsible entity shall retain a copy of the receipted IRS schedule 1 (form 2290), or an acceptable substitute prescribed by 26 CFR part 4 sec 41.6001.-2 for a period of 1 year.
Grace Period
*July 1- September 30th
*Gives carriers 90 days to get their HVUT forms and payments to the IRS and time for the IRS to return stamped/watermarked official 2290
*New vehicles can register for the full year but can only purchase GVW at 55,000 LBS or more for the first month of registration period until a valid 2290 is provided.
HVUT is a free assessed annually on heavy vehicles operation on public highways at registered gross weights equal to or exceeding 55,000 LBS.
*Exempt groups-Do not have to file
**Federal Government
**State or Local Government
**American Red Cross
**Nonprofits
**Indian Tribal Governments (for vehicles used in essential tribal government functions)
**Mass transportation Authorities
How to register a Homemade Trailer?
Have a homemade trailer that you would like to register?? Come into the Hill County Treasurer's office and get a VIN assignment for $5.15 and the following documents need to be completed, MV39T: Certificate of trailer number assignment, MV1: Application for vehicle title.
Once the VIN assignment has been assigned, VIN has been stamped or welded to trailer, VIN inspection done by local law enforcement has been done, come in with completed documents and we can get you registered!
Bought a vehicle and DON'T have a title?
If you have purchased a vehicle and don't have the title? We have way to help!!
Break/Bond is the way to go!!
MV10: Application for Break/Bond: Requires a Notary
MV20-1: Level 1 VIN inspection: Local law enforcement inspection
MV24: Bill of Sale: Requires a Notary
Once all three documents have been completed, a fee of $12.36 is required will supporting documents are to be sent to the state at PO Box 201431 Helena, MT 59620.
Need help through the process? Come in and we can assist you in the right direction!
