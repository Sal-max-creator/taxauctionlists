# Disclaimer

TaxAuctionLists provides general information about government tax lien and tax deed sales. It is **information only, not investment, financial or legal advice**, and it creates no professional relationship.

Sale dates, deposits, deadlines, interest rates, redemption periods and procedures are set by each county and state and can change or be cancelled at any time, often with little notice. Properties are frequently withdrawn before a sale. We check our sources regularly, but we can't promise that any listing is complete, current or correct.

Before you register, deposit money or bid, confirm every detail directly with the county office that runs the sale, read its official terms of sale, and consider talking to a licensed attorney or other qualified professional in that state. Buying at a tax sale carries real risk, including losing your money.

Links to county, state and auction websites are provided for convenience. We don't control those sites and aren't responsible for their content.
